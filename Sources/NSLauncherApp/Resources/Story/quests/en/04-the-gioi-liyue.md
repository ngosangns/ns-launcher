# World Quest — Liyue (including The Chasm: Underground Mines)

*All of the Liyue region's world quests: the major chains, standalone
quests, Random Events and Reputation Requests.*

Liyue has the second-densest concentration of World Quests in the game,
and it is also the region where world quests carry the most lore
relative to the Archon Quest's share: the two major chains (`The Chasm
Delvers`, `Chenyu's Blessings of Sunken Jade`) both open up settings
that the main storyline only mentions in passing — the Cataclysm 500
years ago, the ancient contracts between the Adepti and humanity, and
the price of "immortality". The rest is a very dense picture of daily
life: clerks chasing paperwork, photographers, chefs, merchants,
miners.

The list below follows the **Liyue / The Chasm: Underground Mines /
Random Events / Requests** sections of the wiki's `World Quest` page.
**107** named quests in total. Quest names are kept in English because
they serve as lookup identifiers.

---

## The Chasm Delvers (major chain)

The Ministry of Civil Affairs dispatches Muning to investigate the
anomalies at The Chasm — the mining area the Qixing sealed off with the
"Seven-Star Seal". An official exploration team is formed, including
the young engineer Zhiqiong. After breaking the Bedrock Keys to open
the way down into the mines, the team sets up camp and the Traveler
uses the Lumenstone Adjuvant to light the dark zones, while cleansing
the "black mud" (Oozing Concretion) spreading Abyssal Corruption. The
deeper they go, the more is revealed: ancient ruins, a colossal
machine, and the origin of the black mud — a fragment of a "Skyfrost
Nail" fallen from Celestia that the Abyss Order has corrupted. The
Traveler purifies that celestial nail, the black mud recedes and the
region's earthquakes settle down. Zhiqiong, however, does not stop —
she stays behind to venture deeper into the Abyss-tainted zone, her
fate left open.

## Valor's Afterglow (chain, sequel to The Chasm Delvers)

After the crisis at The Chasm closes, Zhiqiong continues her survey
work underground. The Traveler helps her place data-collection beacons,
then next time must go rescue her when she retrieves the beacons alone
and gets surrounded in the Stony Halls. By the third time, Zhiqiong has
vanished entirely, leaving only a letter saying she will go deeper
still. This chain is the personal epilogue for the character Zhiqiong:
its themes are the fear of being forgotten and the price of adventurous
ambition.

## Lost in a Foreign Land (chain)

Down in the mines, the Traveler meets Katarina Snezhevna — a Fatui
mage abandoned underground so long that her supply line has been cut
and she has never even heard of the Traveler. Over several
conversations, she reveals that everything the Fatui do is preparation
for a "final battle" against a common enemy and an unavoidable long
night. She asks the Traveler to find her comrade Nikolay, and all that
turns up is his badge and a few journal scraps. By the next day,
Katarina herself has also vanished, leaving Nikolay's letter beside a
Shadowy Husk. This chain is a rare slice looking at the Fatui from the
side of the soldiers left behind.

| 2 | Lost in a Foreign Land: Seeking |

## Chenyu's Blessings of Sunken Jade (major chain)

Upon arriving at Chenyu Vale, the Traveler is drawn into an adeptal
realm and meets Fujin — the god governing the vale's waters and soil.
Fujin explains that the water veins and soil are failing, threatening
Qiaoying Village, which lives off tea. Following the trail of the
declining tea quality, the Traveler passes through Yilong Wharf and
hears again the legend of the Jademouth jade ring and the golden carp
that once threw jade to dam the Bishui River. The true cause turns out
to be Lingyuan — a Solitary Suanni disguised as "Bluey" — trying to
break the spirit veins to return the vale to its primal state before
humans arrived. After the confrontation, Lingyuan agrees to let Fujin
perform the Rainjade Rite; Fujin, exhausted, returns to her carp form,
while Lingyuan leaves open the question of what she thinks of humanity.

## Hereafter (chain)

A continuation of Xiao's Story Quest. On the way to bring offerings to
the temple of Pervases — a deceased Yaksha — the Traveler runs into
Wang Ping'an again, the man who once ran scams under the name
"Starsnatcher", now reformed and wanting to rebuild the derelict
temple with his own hands. The Traveler clears the Treasure Hoarders
harassing the worksite, gathers materials, and after a few days the
temple is fully restored. Wang Ping'an stays there writing a book about
the guardian Yaksha. Theme: the Yakshas kept the peace for Liyue
without demanding anyone's gratitude, and the remembrance comes from
someone who once did wrong.

## The Chasm: Underground Mines

The underground mines are split into their own section on the wiki.
Besides the three chains already covered above (`The Chasm Delvers`,
`Valor's Afterglow`, `Lost in a Foreign Land`), there are seven
standalone quests — mostly revolving around the exploration camp, the
scholar Khedive and the missing miners.

**Khedive's survey group** — three underground natural-research quests,
of the collect-and-report kind:

| Quest name | Description |
|---|---|
| Hydrological Investigation in The Chasm | Place devices by the water veins, activate them with a Hydro reaction and defeat the Hydro Geovishap that burrows up. |
| Mycological Investigation in The Chasm | Collect 8 Starshrooms and bring them back to Khedive for appraisal. |
| Paleontological Investigation in The Chasm | Photograph the fossils in the mines and send them to Khedive. |

**The people and creatures down below** — the remaining four quests:

| Quest name | Description |
|---|---|
| A Company Vanishing Into the Deep | Encounter a Fatui squad stranded in the depths; you can fight them or supply them with rations. |
| Dimming Mushroom's Call for Help | Use the Lumenstone Adjuvant to recharge and cleanse Oozing Concretion for Xamaran and its kin. |
| The Chasm's Bounty | Search for the missing miner Qi Ding, following a doll and a strange voice in the dark. |
| The Missing Miner | Hidden quest (unlocked via the "Work Handbook" inside a log in the Underground Waterway): pick "meat mushrooms" with Uncle He, then bring him back to camp. |

## Requests (Reputation Requests — Liyue)

Unlocked after completing `Ministry Missions` and Archon Quest Chapter
1, Act 3: `A New Star Approaches`. These are weekly requests from
ordinary Liyue citizens, each granting 80 Liyue Reputation EXP on
completion. In essence they are all "bring me a few of these things" —
their value lies in sketching the region's everyday life, not in story.

**Food and kitchen ingredients group:**

| Smiley's Selections | Smiley Yanxiao at Wangshu Inn — Ham / Sausage / Bacon for devising new dishes. |
| Yi Zhu's Snack | Yi Zhu — one serving of Almond Tofu. |

## Scope Notes

- This list follows the four Liyue sections of the `World Quest` page.
  The wiki files **event** quests (Lantern Rite, Moonchase Festival,
  Theater Mechanicus, Divine Ingenuity, the Vibro-Crystal / Spike
  Self-Circulation Report chains...) under a separate "Event List" tab;
  they are outside this file's scope even though they also carry the
  Liyue Quests tag.
- `Stygian Onslaught` and `Witch's Homework` are not quests themselves
  but game modes / systems; only the World Quests belonging to them
  that appear in the Liyue section are listed here.
- Liyue has no "Repeatable Random Quests" section on the `World Quest`
  page (only Mondstadt, Inazuma and Sumeru do).
- The wiki does not provide story summaries for most standalone quests;
  the descriptions in the table above are condensed from each quest
  page's Steps section.
