# World Quest — Inazuma

*A catalog of every world quest across the Inazuma archipelago, from
the ritual cleansing of the sacred sakura to the sunken kingdom of
Enkanomiya.*

Inazuma has the densest concentration of World Quests among the game's
first three regions. Most quests here tie directly into the aftermath
of the Sakoku Decree and the civil war between the Shogunate and the
Watatsumi resistance: the perpetual thunderstorm, Tatarigami
corruption, cursed islands, and the remains of dead gods. This document
lists every permanent World Quest in Inazuma the way the wiki groups
them: the major chains, standalone quests, the separate Enkanomiya
region, plus Random Events, Repeatable Random Quests and Reputation
Requests.

In total: **87** World Quests on Inazuma's surface + **20** in
Enkanomiya, along with 3 Random Events, 4 Repeatable Random Quests and
15 Reputation Requests.

## Ritou Travel Permit

The opening chain right after the Traveler lands. Because of the Sakoku
Decree, foreigners are held on Ritou island and cannot proceed without
a permit. Kanjou Commissioner Hiiragi Shinsuke acts welcoming but has
no intention of issuing one, so the Traveler turns to a private deal
with his daughter Hiiragi Chisato: deliver a letter to her lover Kujou
Kamaji of the Tenryou Commission in exchange for the permit. Although
it appears as its own chapter in the quest journal, this chain contains
exactly one quest.

| # | Quest name | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chisato's Letter | Deliver the letter to Kujou Kamaji, receive the permit, then bring the reply letter back to Chisato. |

## Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual

The chain that explains the core mechanic of Inazuma's faith. The roots
of the Sacred Sakura and the Thunder Sakura are interconnected, drawing
up the land's "filth"; if not cleansed periodically, that filth fester
into tumors and the sacred tree will wither. In Konda Village, the
Traveler meets a masked shrine maiden named Hanachirusato and accepts
the task of breaking five seals around the tree roots, destroying the
filth condensed into monsters at each site. After destroying the tumor
beneath the Depths of Mt. Yougou, the truth emerges: Hanachirusato is
herself filth condensed from the memories of Kitsune Saiguu, so for the
ritual to be complete she too must fade away. This chain plants
important lore markers for Yae Miko and the Grand Narukami Shrine.

| # | Quest name | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A Strange Story in Konda | Investigate the strange happenings in Konda Village, descend into the dried-up well and meet Hanachirusato. |
| 2 | Sacrificial Offering | Go to the abandoned shrine, obtain the Memento Lens at the Grand Narukami Shrine to read the ritual traces. |
| 3 | Cleansing Defilement | Perform the cleansing portions at Araumi, the Kamisato Estate and Chinju Forest. |
| 3b | Tanuki-Bayashi in the Forest | Side branch in Chinju Forest: play with the tanuki pack so Ioroi agrees to hand over the ward. |
| 4 | Yougou Cleansing | Descend into Mt. Yougou, destroy the "Miasmic Tumor" and bid Hanachirusato farewell. |

## Orobashi's Legacy

The chain explaining why Yashiori Island's rain and storms never cease.
The scholar Kaji at Fort Fujitou proposes a hypothesis: the abnormal
weather stems from damaged ancient wards once used to suppress what
remains of the serpent god Orobashi. Orobashi was the guardian deity of
Watatsumi Island, one who learned a forbidden truth about the world
that Celestia wanted hidden, then deliberately led an army against
Yashiori to be slain by the Raiden Shogun — that strike carved out
Musoujin Gorge and scattered the god's remains across the island,
giving rise to the Tatarigami phenomenon. The Traveler restores four
wards in turn; after the first three, Fort Mumei rises from the sea,
where the final great ward is guarded by a Maguu Kenki. Once repaired,
the rain and lightning over Yashiori come to an end.

| # | Quest name | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Orobashi's Legacy: Prologue | Meet Kaji and hear the hypothesis about the wards suppressing the serpent god. |
| 2 | Orobashi's Legacy: Part I | Repair the ward at Musoujin Gorge. |
| 3 | Orobashi's Legacy: Part II | Find the missing piece of the ward at Serpent's Head. |
| 4 | Orobashi's Legacy: Part III | Repair the ward inside Jakotsu Mine; unlocks the Formation Estate domain. |
| 5 | Orobashi's Legacy: Part IV | Report to Kaji after Fort Mumei surfaces. |
| 6 | Orobashi's Legacy: Part V | Defeat the Maguu Kenki at Fort Mumei, repair the final ward, receive the Kitain Cross Spear diagram. |

## Tatara Tales

Several years ago, the Tenryou Commission hired Fontaine engineers to
build the Mikage Furnace to boost Jade Steel output, choosing to feed
the furnace with Tatarigami energy itself. When the civil war broke
out, that energy source spun out of control, pitting the soldier
Toranosuke and the shrine maiden Miyuki against each other. The
Traveler seeks out Xavier — a Fontaine advisor who stayed behind in
Inazuma — and through investigation discovers the Fatui are the root
of the incident. The chain spans seven real days (each quest unlocks
after a daily reset) with stages of surveying, placing beacons,
collecting data and repairing paths, before the two make a final trip
into the contaminated zone to purify the Tatarigami core.

| # | Quest name | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tatara Tales | Observe the Mikage Furnace, fire the Kamuijima Cannon to break the rifts, photograph the storage devices. |
| 2 | Tatara Tales: Priority Investigation | Place three beacons around the furnace area. |
| 3 | Tatara Tales: Purification Device | Gather Crystal Marrow for the purification device. |
| 4 | Tatara Tales: Data Collection | Retrieve the beacons already placed. |
| 5 | Tatara Tales: Process Is Everything | Photograph the furnace's current state for the report. |

## Seirai Stormchasers

The Adventurers' Guild hits a wall surveying Seirai Island because
Balethunder blocks every route to the island's center. The two
adventurers Etsuko and Taisuke only manage to find a few broken
"Warding Stones" without knowing what to do with them. The Traveler
follows cat pawprints to Asase Shrine and meets Neko — a talking cat
calling herself the shrine's "provisional head priestess", still
keeping the shrine clean every day for fear her former owner Hibiki
would be sad upon returning. In exchange for repairing the offering
box, Neko teaches how to seal the four Warding Stones; the last two
float in the sky and can only be reached via Phase Gates. Once sealed,
a colossal stone platform appears in midair, where the Traveler defeats
the Thunder Manifestation and picks up the Peculiar Pinion — the
strange feather that opens the way to the Tsurumi Island chain.

| # | Quest name | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seirai Stormchasers: Part I | Meet Etsuko, follow the cat tracks to Asase Shrine, repair the offering box and seal the first stone. |
| 2 | Seirai Stormchasers: Part II | Seal the second Warding Stone. |
| 3 | Seirai Stormchasers: Part III | Find a way up the floating rock platforms to reach the third stone. |
| 4 | Seirai Stormchasers: Part IV | Seal the final stone, defeat the Thunder Manifestation, pick up the Peculiar Pinion. |

## Neko Is a Cat

The gentle sequel chain to Seirai Stormchasers, consisting of nine
small quests done one per day to restore Asase Shrine. Neko believes
the prettier the shrine, the more visitors will come and the sooner
Hibiki will return, so she asks in turn for cleaning the shrine,
repairing the prayer rack, calling the stray kittens home, cooking the
dish Hibiki used to make, then carving a stone statue of herself.
Meanwhile Etsuko grows suspicious: the islanders evacuated long ago and
no one has visited the shrine in years — Neko simply doesn't know
Hibiki is dead. The most bitter detail comes when the two adventurers
reveal that Domeki, the friend who used to visit Hibiki, was actually a
wanted pirate.

## Through the Mists

The main chain of Tsurumi Island, continuing from Seirai Stormchasers.
The novelist Sumida offers a bounty to whoever can bring a Maushiro
back from the fog-shrouded island, but everyone who takes the job loses
the item the moment they leave. On the island, the Traveler meets the
boy Ruu preparing a ceremony — even though, according to Sumida, the
Tsurumi civilization died out thousands of years ago. The truth,
revealed by the boatman Kama: long ago Ruu willingly offered himself to
the Thunderbird Kanna Kapatcir, but that act enraged the bird god, who
cursed the whole island to relive the ceremony and its disaster
forever. Since Kapatcir was slain by the Raiden Shogun, the Traveler
lies that the bird god merely moved to Seirai, then uses the Peculiar
Pinion to take Ruu there to sing the old song once more — the curse
lifts, the fog recedes, and Ruu is finally released.

| # | Quest name | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A Particularly Particular Author | Take Sumida's job, ride Kama's boat to the island, make offerings at the three Perch trees. |
| 2 | Octave of the Maushiro | Use the Peculiar Pinion to see through illusions, dig up the real Maushiro and search for Ruu. |
| 3 | The Sea of Fog and the Rite of the Trees | Hear the truth from Kama, destroy all the Perches to stop the ceremony. |
| 4 | The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna | Climb Mt. Kanna, defeat the Thunder Manifestation, take Ruu to Seirai to sing and lift the curse. |

The follow-up quest *The Saga of Mr. Forgetful* (listed under
standalone World Quests) and a series of Hidden Exploration Objectives
on the island.

## The Moon-Bathed Deep

The chain that opens the way down to Enkanomiya. The shrine maiden
Tsuyuko at Sangonomiya Shrine explains the Holy Soil phenomenon: the
Enkanomiya depths are draining Watatsumi Island's life force, bleaching
the soil until nothing can grow. To stop it requires a Jade Coral
Branch for the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri ritual, and to descend for it
requires breaking the barrier separating the two realms. That barrier
is anchored to five Goshou Rocks named after parts of the divine
serpent's body — the "eye", "fang", "fin", "tail" and "heart". After
collecting four Spirit Pearls and unlocking the "heart" node, the
Traveler receives the Key of the Deep.

clearer, while running back and forth consulting Liyue's chefs. After
having to rescue the whole group from Kairagi and Nobushi, they throw a

chain also has a Sumeru continuation plus five related Commissions,

## The Very Special Fortune Slip

A post-Archon Quest chain revealing the operations of the Shuumatsuban
— the Yashiro Commission's intelligence branch. Starting from a strange
fortune slip of Gendou Ringo's, the Traveler is pulled into a campaign
hunting Fatui spies inside Inazuma City, then discovers a fake "Gendou"
operating at night. The impostor is the agent Lyudochka; rather than
eliminating her, the organization chooses a softer handling. The finale
is a rescue operation freeing Momoyo from a Fatui prison, requiring
Adventure Rank 40 to enter the domain.

## Film Notes

A comedy chain about the Fontaine director Xavier, unlocked after he
finishes his work at the Mikage Furnace. Unhappy with the film being
shot, Xavier asks the Yashiro Commission for support; Kamisato Ayato
agrees and sends Koharu to help. The Traveler is cast as the lead, but
the first shoot goes so badly that the two actors Sasano and Koharu
quit. Only after the Uyuu Restaurant owner Okazaki Rikuto reads the
script and gives feedback does Xavier rewrite the whole thing, re-invite
the actors and finish the final scene at Nazuchi Beach.

| # | Quest name | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Outlander Brigade!" | Star in Xavier's film, witness the crew falling apart, then have dinner at Uyuu Restaurant. |
| 2 | Mushounin | Re-invite Sasano and Koharu, reshoot with the new script and complete the film. |

## Enkanomiya

Enkanomiya — whose true name is Byakuyakoku — is the underground
kingdom beneath Watatsumi Island, where humans once fled to escape the
Bathysmal Vishaps. With no sun, they built the Dainichi Mikoshi, an
artificial light machine producing the Whitenight and Evernight cycle.
All 20 permanent World Quests here revolve around re-reading that
history through "afterimages" — Sinshades, the lingering shades of the
dead. Entry requirement: complete *The Still Water's Flow*.

### From Dusk to Dawn in Byakuyakoku

The region's main chain. The Traveler descends into Enkanomiya seeking
Bloodbranch Coral and meets Enjou, who claims to be a "clerical
assistant" helping with the trials. He guides the way to the Golden
Bridle in the library to break the barrier around the Dainichi Mikoshi,
then activates the machine, bringing Evernight crashing down. A
Sinshade named Aru gives the next task: gather three fragments to
restore the Reins of Revival, which can make the souls in Evernight
speak. Aru also warns that Enjou is not from Watatsumi — and indeed, at
the tower's base he reveals his true form as an Abyss Lector seeking
records predating the tower's construction. Before retreating in
defeat, he plants a barb suggesting Paimon will one day betray the
Traveler.

### Erebos' Secret

After delivering the Bloodbranch Coral to Tsuyuko, the Traveler meets a
Sinshade named Eboshi, a shrine maiden of Enkanomiya. She opens the
three Towers of the Three Realms once hidden by secret arts —
Byakuyakoku sits on the boundary of three realms: the human realm, the
elemental realm (the Vishaps) and the Abyss realm, so these towers were
built to keep balance among them. Clearing all three trials, the
Traveler hears Eboshi recount the Bathysmal Vishaps, Watatsumi Omikami,
and the time before there was a sun or a moon.

### Hyperion's Dirge

Enkanomiya's most tragic chain. Aberaku calls himself the
"Helios-Forger" — the one who built the Dainichi Mikoshi, the sun
chariot that drove back the Dragonheirs of the Depths and saved the
kingdom. But once peace returned, the nobles turned worship of the
machine into a political tool and raised the Sunchildren as puppets;
Aberaku objected, was framed for treason, died in prison, and his body
and relics were divided among several shrines, leaving his soul unable
to pass on. The most horrifying detail: every Sunchild was burned in a
ritual upon coming of age, so they would never realize they were mere
puppets. The player can start the chain from any of the four quests.

| 3 | Temple Inquiry | Recover the crown enshrined in the Evernight Temple. |
| 4 | Serpent's Heart Inquiry | Recover the ceremonial robes enshrined in The Serpent's Heart. |
