# Hangout Events — Companion Quests

*Replayable slices of everyday life, where each dialogue choice leads to a
different ending.*

Hangout Events (also known as **Invitation Quests**) are a separate branch
of Story Quests, introduced in Version 1.4. Unlike regular Story Quests —
played once along a single storyline — Hangout Events can be replayed
freely, branching on dialogue choices and opening multiple different endings
for the same character. The content leans toward slice-of-life and comedy,
thickening a character's portrait rather than pushing the main plot.

To date, the wiki records **12 Series**, **18 Acts** across **17 characters**
(Noelle alone has two Acts), totaling **102 endings** and roughly **140
sub-quests**. Series XII (Lynette) is the most recent addition, from Version
4.5.

## Mechanics

### Story Keys — the keys that open branches

- Story Keys are unlock items used exclusively for Hangout Events.
- You earn **1 Story Key for every 8 Daily Commission reward claims** (about
  2 days of completing all 4 commissions). Keys don't enter your bag
  automatically: you must claim them manually in the Hangout Events tab of
  the Story Quests screen.
- You can hold at most **3 Story Keys** at once, but the 8-commission
  counter keeps running — spend one key and you can claim the next right
  away.
- Each **Act** costs **2 Story Keys** to unlock. Replaying that Act (to get
  other endings) **costs no additional keys**.
- Regular Story Quests dropped the Story Key requirement in Version 5.4;
  Tribal Chronicles don't need them either. Story Keys now serve only
  Hangout Events.

### Unlock Requirements

Each Act demands three groups of conditions at once:

1. A minimum **Adventure Rank** (lowest is AR 26, highest is AR 40).
2. **Prerequisite quests** — usually an Archon Quest act of the
   corresponding region, and for later characters their own Story Quest as
   well.
3. **2 Story Keys**.

Noelle's Act II alone has an extra special requirement: you must unlock
**every ending** of Act I first.

### Branching and the Heartbeat Value

- Dialogue choices decide where the story goes. Major branch points are
  saved as **checkpoints**, letting you return to that exact fork to take
  the other branch without replaying from the start.
- Each quest has a hidden stat called the **Heartbeat Value** (affection),
  rising and falling with how the player answers — though not every quest
  actually uses it. If this bar empties completely, the quest **fails**.
- Since Version 2.2, the interface shows how many endings have been
  unlocked and marks previously chosen dialogue options. Version 3.8 added
  a review button ("Review Invitation"), and Version 4.5 added the ability
  to skip already-seen dialogue.

### Rewards

- Rewards scale with the **number of endings** unlocked, not the number of
  runs. Each new ending pays out its own bundle.
- A bundle typically contains: Adventure EXP, **Primogems** (at the major
  milestones), Hero's Wit, Talent level-up materials, character ascension
  gems, and that character's **specialty dish**.
- There's also the **Memories of the Heart** achievement group: usually each
  Hangout has one 20-Primogem achievement for unlocking every ending, plus
  two hidden 5-Primogem achievements for minor actions during play.
- Unlocked ending pictures can be displayed in the Serenitea Pot via the
  **Lingering Moment** furnishing (a frame whose blueprint is bought at the
  Realm Depot); guests visiting in Co-Op mode can view them too.

## Series I — Version 1.4

### Barbara — Act I: *Wellspring of Healing*

Sister Victoria is worried because Barbara has disappeared and asks the
Traveler to find her. The story revolves around picking Wolfhooks in
Wolvendom, dodging the fan club led by Albert, and choosing between
Barbara's homemade Chilibrew or a new drink at the Cat's Tail. The
ingredient-hunting branch leads to a Treasure Hoarder harassing merchants,
and depending on how the injured are handled, Barbara may or may not get to
show off her signature dish.

Sub-quests:

```
Sister Victoria's Vexation
├── The Church's Affairs
│   ├── The First Chilibrew Ingredient
│   │   └── Knight and Deaconess, Ready for Battle!
│   ├── The Second Chilibrew Ingredient
│   │   ├── Healing Is My Job
│   │   └── Ulterior Motive...?
│   └── A Very Special Beverage
└── Sudden Shouting
```

Endings (5): *Untimely Enthusiasm* · *A Fiery Flavor From Liyue* · *The Taste
of Hard Work* · *Incompletely Cured* · *A Holiday in Mondstadt*.

### Noelle — Act I: *Chivalric Training*

Noelle asks the Traveler to coach her through the "chivalric training" that
Knights of Favonius candidates undergo. Two root branches: advise her that
she doesn't have to become a knight (leading to a reception for the Liyue
merchant Alois — where the Traveler's reactions can expose his scheme to
copy Dandelion Wine), or actually get to work training her strength and
service etiquette.

Sub-quests:

```
Maid-in-Training
├── Fleeting Leisure
│   └── Gift and Intent
├── Noelle's Strength
│   ├── Strength Training
│   └── Live Practice
└── The Art of Hospitality
```

Endings (6): *A Cold Reception* · *"Goodbye, Miss Maid!"* · *A Defender's Will
Is Their Strength* · *A Maid Above Ground* · *A Conundrum Called Love* ·
*Whisper of the Paper Rose*.

### Chongyun — Act I: *Signs of Evil*

Chongyun — the young exorcist of Liyue Harbor — pulls the Traveler into
hunting "signs of evil", including a flyer advertising a test of courage at
Wuwang Hill. The skeptical branch leads to meeting two poor brothers and a
popsicle-sharing story; the other goes straight into the Test of Courage,
where Chongyun's "excessively yang" constitution keeps ruining every
exorcism ritual.

Sub-quests:

    ├── Sudden Discomfort
    └── Ever Deeper
        └── Killjoy
            └── Independent Actions
```

Endings (6): *Blessing In Disguise* · *Idle Amusements* · *Can't Beat This
Heat* · *Nothing Gained* · *Goodbyes* · *Small Regrets*.

### Bennett — Act I: *Fantastic Voyage*

Bennett asks to tag along with the Traveler, and the player picks one of
three kinds of "adventure": strolling around Mondstadt, hiking in the wilds,
or exploring a new ruin. The whole Act is a set of variations on Bennett's
legendary bad luck — shops sold out, restaurants refusing service, coupons
expiring the very day you arrive, a dandelion meadow picked clean, a
treasure chest containing only vegetables.

Sub-quests:

```

├── What Is This, a Day Trip?
├── Treacherous Thermadon

Royce* · *Ready to Make Sacrifices* · *No Wonder It's a Top-Class Commission*
· *Fortunate Outcome*.

## Series II — Version 1.5

### Diona — Act I: *The Cat and the Cocktail*

It starts with a conversation with Nimrod, then moves to the Cat's Tail bar.
Diona — the little bartender who hates alcohol but has a heaven-sent talent
for mixing it — pulls the Traveler into catching a stray cat and hunting
ingredients for a "special" base drink, including a trip into Dadaupa Gorge
to cook a Samachurl herbal soup.

Sub-quests:

```
The Cat's Tail's Bartender
├── To Catch a Kitten
├── A Special Base Drink
│   └── The Shadow Over Dadaupa
│       └── Samachurl Herbal Soup
└── Special Cocktailing Condiments
    └── Exchanging Pointers
```

Endings (5): *Cat Party* · *The Unfathomable Felines* · *Diona's Special* ·
*"I... Only Had... a Little..."* · *The Ultimate Special*.

### Noelle — Act II: *Knightly Exam Prep*

After the trip to Liyue, the Traveler finds Noelle buried in the library
cramming for the knight exam. Three main branches: review the Liyue trip
with her (meeting the Millelith, logging the journey), take the Adventurers'
Guild entrance exam, or send Noelle "into the field" in acting and
reconnaissance roles. This is the only Act that requires unlocking all of
Act I's endings first.

Sub-quests:

```
Studious Maid
├── Journey to Liyue
│   ├── While the Millelith Stands Guard
│   └── A Knight's Journey Through Liyue
├── Adventurer's Entrance Exam
└── Noelle Rides Out
    ├── Substitute Acting Grand Master Noelle
    └── Noelle the Intelligence Operative
```

Endings (6): *Rest Amidst the Rocks* · *"The Maid-Knight's Tale"* · *Perfect
Score* · *Points Deducted* · *The One and Only Noelle* · *Work Made for a
Maid*.

the city and asks a miko which way she went. Thanking her and leaving it at
that branches into helping Sayu find ways to "grow taller" and dodge work;
continuing the investigation branches into suspicions about the
Shuumatsuban itself. Recurring themes: sleep, height, and the expectations
placed on a tiny ninja.

## Series IV — Version 2.3

### Gorou — Act I: *The Canine General's Special Operations*

Gorou is suffering because of Yae Miko. The branch tree is quite wide from
the start: either advise him to ignore Yae (then split further into staying
with his buddies, watching over the Yae Publishing House, or avoiding her
entirely), or help him overcome his weakness through theory and drills with
the personality type he fears most. The final branch touches memories of
those who fell in the Watatsumi war.

Sub-quests:

├── A Friend's Path

*The Smiling Send-Off* · *Final Remembrance*.

### Beidou — Act I: *When the Crux Shines Bright*

The Traveler boards The Alcor to meet Beidou. Expressing interest in a
sailor's life leads into "Sailor Training" (choosing between the Crux's
Shield or Spear); thanking the crew instead shifts to a private chat,
choosing Guili Plains or Qingce Village for a treasure hunt. The Guili
Plains branch ties into an Inazuman sailor's homesickness.

Sub-quests:

finish it opens the Wisdom Seelie branch and an investigation chain;
