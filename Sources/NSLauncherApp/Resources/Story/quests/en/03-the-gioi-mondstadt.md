# World Quest — Mondstadt (including Dragonspine)

*The land of wind and freedom: from everyday chores in the storm's
aftermath, across a snow-capped mountain that buried an entire kingdom,
to the homework of witches.*

Mondstadt's World Quests fall into two clear layers. The first is the
everyday errands around the city — cleaning up after the Stormterror
attack, helping the blacksmith find ore, helping the little flower girl
pick up the basket the wind scattered. The second layer is where the
lore lives: Dragonspine with its ice-entombed city, Bough Keeper:
Dainsleif opening the door to Khaenri'ah, and the Witch's Homework
chain that brings an entire generation of witches back onto the stage.

This document follows the exact sections of the wiki's "World Quest"
page: the Mondstadt section, the Dragonspine section, plus four
sub-groups (Random Events, Repeatable Random Quests, Crimson Wish,
Reputation Requests). Quests that only exist within limited-time events
are not in this list — the wiki files them under a separate "Event
List" tab.

## Series: Witch's Homework

Mondstadt's largest series, and also an in-game system. The Traveler
receives a surprise "commission" from the Knights of Favonius — which
turns out to have been passed along by Lisa — and comes home with
**The Little Witch's Dictionary**, the witch Alice's dictionary. Each
dictionary page is an assignment Alice gave to one of her acquaintances,
and the Traveler becomes the one delivering the homework. Each sub-quest
is a character vignette: Venti singing with the wind, Klee overcoming a
"trial" set by her mother, Mona dragged all the way to the gates of
Khaenri'ah, Durin stepping into the human world for the first time,
Varka returning to Mondstadt, Razor holding the wolf pack against the
monsters of the Abyss. Completing each quest unlocks the Witch's Eve
Rite Passive for the corresponding character, so this series is both
story and combat mechanic — on the condition that you own that
character at level 70 or above.

## Series: A Long Day in the Mountains

A series marked as a "Major World Quest Chain". At Dornman Port, a
patroller named Mann recounts meeting a "ghost" at the abandoned
sanatorium on Windrest Peak: a young woman standing before a grave that
bears her own face. The Traveler tracks her down and meets Zeitlind, who
is searching for her missing sister in the sanatorium — that gravestone
belongs to the sister. Mid-investigation, the sky turns red and an
angel-shaped machine calling itself Watcher Nihilita appears, imprisons
Zeitlind, then asks the Traveler to go to the Temple of Space to free
the temple from its "reversed fate", entrusting them with a young
Watcher named Dex. Inside the temple, Istoria explains the place was
created by the God of Space; after that god vanished, the remaining
Watcher Melanta seized power and terrorized the inhabitants. The series
title borrows from Thomas Mann's novel *The Magic Mountain*, and most
of the chain takes place inside the Temple of Space rather than on the
Mondstadt map.

| Chapter 1 — Paradise | Long Live Life |

| Chapter 1 — Paradise | Unofficial Chronicle of the Desert Pavilion |
| Chapter 2 — Hell | Lion's Celerity |

Only the Prologue act (*The Wind Has Ceased*) is listed by the wiki

Space area.

## Series: Battlefield of Dice, Cats, and Cards

The tutorial chain that unlocks Genius Invokation TCG. Sucrose receives
a strange parcel from an Akademiya researcher and is dissecting it with
Timaeus to figure out what the cards inside are for. The Traveler
follows the trail to The Cat's Tail tavern, meets Margaret, then learns
the rules through three practice matches against Diona, Sucrose and
Fischl. Win all three and you receive the Casket of Tomes plus a starter
deck, and the TCG mode opens up for the entire world. Requirements:
Adventure Rank 30 and completion of *Song of the Dragon and Freedom*.

|---|---|
| 1 | Come Try Genius Invokation TCG! |

## Chapter I: Act IV - Prelude — Bough Keeper: Dainsleif

Technically this is a single World Quest, but the game files it under
"Chapter I: Act IV - Prelude" and gives it full voice acting like an
Archon Quest. Katheryne reports that a stranger has been asking about
the Traveler; following the accounts of Lawrence, Wagner and Charles,
the Traveler meets Dainsleif at the tavern. He hires the Traveler for
500 Mora — a symbolic figure for the 500 years since the Cataclysm —
then leads them through the Temple of the Falcon, Wolvendom and
Stormterror's Lair, the locations tied to the Four Winds that Barbatos
charged with guarding Mondstadt. The trip exposes traces of the Abyss
Order and paves the way for the entire Khaenri'ah storyline to come.
Requirement: complete *A New Star Approaches*.

| Order | Quest |
|---|---|
| 1 | Bough Keeper: Dainsleif |

## Dragonspine

The snow-capped Dragonspine is a sub-region of Mondstadt, with its own
Sheer Cold mechanic and its own Frostbearing Tree. The story here
revolves around an ancient kingdom buried under ice and the death of
the dragon Durin.

**In the Mountains** — the region's main unlock chain. Iris at the
expedition camp reports abnormally hard ice blocks sealing every path
up the mountain. The Traveler uses Scarlet Quartz to melt each shard,
climbs all the way to the Skyfrost Nail — the celestial nail driven
into the mountain — and along the way unlocks the Frostbearing Tree
offering system. This is the prerequisite for nearly all further
Dragonspine exploration.

**A Land Entombed** — a pure lore thread. The Traveler picks up three
parts of an ancient research journal scattered through Starglow Cavern
and the Entombed City ruins, then brings them to the researcher Stevens
at the base camp. The journal tells of a kingdom that once existed
right beneath this ice before being buried.

**The Festering Fang** — the Traveler picks up a strange dragon's tooth
on the mountain. Orban the blacksmith at the camp needs a Northlander
Polearm Prototype, 50 Starsilver Ore and three more teeth; midway
through, the Fatui jump in to contest the energy source needed to
"activate" the dragon tooth. This is the permanent quest that allows
refining the sword **Festering Desire** — a weapon originally issued
by the event *The Chalk Prince and the Dragon* (2020), and the quest
*Festering Desire* itself is that event's Story Quest rather than a
permanent World Quest.

**Snowy Silhouette (Hope / Reunion)** — the continuation of the story
of the boy Joel searching for his father. Cyrus tracks down a lead,
Reckless Pallad makes an appearance, and the ending is sealed in
*Reunion* when the camp at the mountain's foot welcomes a new guest.
Note: *Hope* is only obtainable if you have not yet completed Albedo's
Story Quest *Shadows Amidst Snowstorms*.

**The Great Mountain Survey I & II** — environmental researcher Esther
lost nearly all her survey beacons when her survey team was attacked by
monsters. Part I has you recover and re-plant four beacons; Part II
requires planting one more beacon right on the summit to measure
Dragonspine's true height, and ends with a letter sent back to Cyrus.

## Random Events (Mondstadt)

Quests that pop up randomly when the Traveler passes a certain spot in
Mondstadt. They vanish if left too long or if you stray too far from
the trigger point. All of them are short combat encounters or monster
structure demolitions, with virtually no dialogue.

| Quest | Group |
|---|---|
| Chubby Crisis | Clear a group of monsters on the spot |
| Dangerous Cluster | Clear a group of monsters on the spot |
| Eliminating the Hidden Danger | Destroy the monsters' training device |
| Increasing Danger (Random Event) | Defeat monsters and destroy the watchtower they erected |
| Prevention Measures | Clear a group of monsters on the spot |
| Strange Mark | Use Anemo to blow away a strange mark, then monsters appear |
| The Prey | Clear a group of monsters on the spot |

## Repeatable Random Quests (Mondstadt)

Same mechanic as Random Events but repeatable. They fall into three
clear groups: **"Dilemma"** — an NPC surrounded by monsters, rescue
them and talk; **"Troubles"** — an NPC needs cooking ingredients, hand
them over and done; **"Transport Request"** — ferry goods back and
forth between two researchers.

## Crimson Wish Quests (Dragonspine)

Dragonspine's own quest group, unlocked when Frostbearing Tree's
Gratitude reaches level 8 and disappearing when the tree reaches level
12. The reward is Crimson Agate — the item offered to the tree. All of
them are short challenges: either timed, or clearing monsters.

| Quest | Content |
|---|---|
| Ancient Wind | Timed challenge: collect 8 Anemo elemental particles within 60 seconds |
| Dredging the Land | Timed challenge: destroy the Frostburst barrels hilichurls piled up blocking the earth vein |
| Icy Harvest | Defeat the monsters occupying a Crimson Agate (some versions require melting ice first) |
| Liquidation | Defeat the Snezhnayan recon squad hunting the mountain's peculiar energy |
| Strange Shifts | Timed challenge: destroy the ancient ruins' automated defense mechanisms |

## Scope Notes

- The list follows the sectioning of the wiki's "World Quest" page as
  of the time of research. Quests that only open during limited-time
  events (Windblume, Windtrace, Mimi Tomo, Of Drink A-Dreaming, The
  Chalk Prince and the Dragon...) live under a separate "Event List"
  tab and are not listed here, even though the wiki still files them
  under Category:Mondstadt Quests.
- The four *Adventure Rank Ascension* quests and *World Level
  Ascension* are actually Ascension Quests shared across the whole
  game, but their location is in Mondstadt so the wiki lists them here.
- The *Witch's Homework* series is still expanding with each version;
  the number of acts may grow as new Hexerei characters arrive.
