# All Archon Quests

*Main storyline: 11 chapters, 48 Acts, 224 sub-quests — updated through
Version 7.0.*

Archon Quests are the backbone of Genshin Impact's story: the Traveler and
Paimon journey through each nation, meet each member of The Seven, and
follow the trail of the sibling stolen away by an unknown god in the
opening scene. This document lists every Chapter, every Act, and every
sub-quest in play order.

**How to read.** Each Chapter is a `##` section, each Act a `###` section
with its unlock conditions. Quest names are kept in English because they
are lookup identifiers. The summary column describes what the player
actually does in each quest, paraphrased rather than quoted from the game.
In-game, some quest names collide with other wiki pages and carry a
`(Quest)` suffix; the in-game display names are used here.

## Prologue: The Outlander Who Caught the Wind

Mondstadt, the nation of wind and freedom, is being ravaged by the dragon
Stormterror. The Traveler joins the Knights of Favonius, then meets a
wandering bard who turns out to be Barbatos — the Anemo Archon. The
chapter closes by revealing two great truths: Stormterror is actually
Dvalin, poisoned by the Abyss, and the Traveler's sibling is on the other
side of the war.

falls ill from a power radiating out of the Armory Palace, and an
underground organization called Rokot is trying to stop the project. The
chapter currently has two Acts, and Chapter VII is still being updated.

## Interlude Chapter

The Interlude Chapter is a between-chapters chapter, not tied to any
specific nation. Its four Acts respectively extend the Liyue thread (the
Jade Chamber and the Adepti), the Chasm thread, Scaramouche's origins, and
finally a case in Mondstadt that leads straight into the Nod-Krai
chapter. It is also where the game places lore pieces that do not fit
neatly into the main chapters.
