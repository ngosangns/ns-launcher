# Story Quest — Character Story Quests

*The complete set of per-character quest chains, sorted by nation and release
order, with the story content each chapter reveals.*

Story Quests are quest chains revolving around a playable character and
their relationship with the Traveler. Three main forms:

- **Character Story Chapter** — a standalone story chapter, named after the
  character's Constellation, split into Acts, each Act containing several
  consecutive sub-quests.
- **Tribal Chronicles** — Natlan's own variant, named after the tribe
  instead of the character, tied to the Tribe Reputation system.
- **Event Story Quest Chapter** — story chapters tied to version events;
  since Version 3.2 they no longer require completing the character's
  story quest first — the game only recommends it.

There are also **Character Hangout Events** (multi-branch dating quests
with multiple endings) — the wiki groups them with Story Quests but their
mechanics differ; here they are only listed, not covered in depth.

**Reading conventions.** The wiki has no dedicated "chapter subtitle"
field: the in-game label consists of the chapter name (in the form `<Name>
Chapter`) and each Act's name, so the "chapter name meaning" notes below
come from the Chinese/Japanese name translations the wiki records; where
the wiki has no translation, the note is left blank. The version noted is
each Act's release version. Chapters are grouped by **character homeland**
according to the wiki's `region` field; when a chapter takes place
elsewhere, a separate note says so. Quest names are kept in English
because they are lookup identifiers.

**Story Keys.** Since Version 5.4, Story Quest Acts no longer cost Story
Keys; Tribal Chronicles are also free. Only Hangout Events still cost 2
Story Keys per Act (replaying the same Hangout costs nothing extra).

**Ties to Archon Quests.** Only a few Story Quests are mandatory
prerequisites for continuing the main storyline: *The Meaning of Lupical*
(Razor, which pulls in Amber's *Wind, Courage, and Wings*) for Chapter I
Act IV; *The Whispers of the Crane and the White Rabbit* (Ayaka) and
*Dreamlike Timelessness* (Yoimiya) for Chapter II Act II; *A Strange and
Friendless Road* (Kazuha, which pulls in Raiden Shogun's *Reflections of
Mortality*) and *Traveler Observation Report* (Albedo) for Act I of Song
of the Welkin Moon.

## Mondstadt

### Lepus Chapter — Amber

*Chapter of the Little Rabbit.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Wind, Courage, and Wings (v1.0) | Glide With the Wind → License to Glide → Mondstadt Glider → They Call Him Raptor → Outrider Style |

The lightest opening chapter in the game: Amber trains the Traveler for
the gliding license exam. Its lore value lies in introducing the Outrider
role — the front-line scouts of the Knights of Favonius — and showing that
Amber holds that title nearly alone after the scout corps declined. It is
also a mandatory prerequisite for Razor's story quest.

### Pavo Ocellus Chapter — Kaeya

*Chapter of the Peacock Feather.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Secret Pirate Treasure (v1.0) | Kaeya's Troubles → Mystery of the Arcadian Ruins → Kaeya's Gain |

Kaeya asks the Traveler to find the "treasure" his grandfather left
behind, but the story soon reveals he was probing the Traveler while
hiding his own affairs. This chapter sketches Kaeya's two-sided nature:
flippant on the outside, always calculating inside, with a family past he
does not speak of — a detail later tied to his Khaenri'ah origins. Kaeya
is also the first character to have both a Story Quest and a Hangout
Event.

### Tempus Fugit Chapter — Lisa

*Chapter of Hourglass.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Troublesome Work (v1.0) | Troublesome Work → Lost Book |

Lisa asks the Traveler to recover overdue books for the Knights of
Favonius library. Her laziness conceals something: Lisa was once the most
outstanding student of the Sumeru Akademiya, and precisely because she saw
the price of knowledge clearly, she deliberately chose a slow life in
Mondstadt. This chapter is the unlock requirement for Xingqiu's story
quest.

### Lupus Minor Chapter — Razor

*Chapter of the Little Wolf.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | The Meaning of Lupical (v1.0) | Wolves' Territory → Another Day as an Outrider! → Ravaged by Wolves → Fate's Chosen Lupical |

The Traveler follows Razor into Wolvendom and learns the meaning of the
word "Lupical" — pack, family. Razor was raised by the wolf pack of
Andrius (Boreas, Lupus Boreas), and this chapter establishes the bond
between the boy and the Wolf of the North. This is a mandatory Story Quest
for continuing Archon Quest Chapter I Act IV.

### Noctua Chapter — Diluc

*Chapter of the Night Owl.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Darknight Hero's Alibi (v1.0) | Legend of the Darknight Hero → Darknight Hero's Crisis → Darknight Hero's Alibi |

Rumors of a "Darknight Hero" meting out justice in the night spread across
Mondstadt, and the Traveler gradually realizes the hero is the master of
Dawn Winery himself. This chapter shows that Diluc left the Knights of
Favonius but not his responsibility: he hunts the Fatui and criminals in
his own way, outside the system.

### Trifolium Chapter — Klee

*Chapter of the Four-Leaf Clover.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | True Treasure (v1.0) | Who Is Mondstadt's Strongest Fighter? → The Mystery of the Girl in Red's Treasure → A Very Volatile Treasure |

The "who is Mondstadt's strongest" argument at Angel's Share leads to a
hunt for Klee's "treasure". The chapter introduces Alice — Klee's mother,
a world-class adventurer/witch and Hexenzirkel member — through the traces
she left behind, while also explaining why Jean has to put Klee in
solitary confinement so often.

### Leo Minor Chapter — Jean

*Chapter of the Lion Cub.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Master's Day Off (v1.0) | When the West Wind Arises → (three quests playable in any order) Margaret's Longing · Charles' Troubles · Sara's Worries → Master's Day Off |

Jean is exhausted from carrying the whole Knights of Favonius machinery,
so the Traveler and the knights force her to take a day off by covering
her daily work. The chapter shows that real power in Mondstadt runs on an
Acting Grand Master working beyond her limits while Grand Master Varka is
away on expedition — the backdrop for nearly the entire Mondstadt arc.

### Carmen Dei Chapter — Venti

*Chapter of the Immortal of Song.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Should You Be Trapped in a Windless Land (v1.0) | A Child's Secret → The South Wind Brings Adventure → Lionfang's Legacy → Should You Be Trapped in a Windless Land |

Venti stages a trick using a prop to "see" imaginary friends, and the
story revolves around memories of the Lawrence Clan and those left behind
in Mondstadt's history. This is a rare chapter showing the Anemo Archon
intervening through story and music rather than raw power — exactly how a
god of freedom operates.

### Astrolabos Chapter — Mona

*The Chapter of Sky-Reflecting.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Beyond This World's Stars (v1.0) | Astrology and the 50-Year Pact → To Mondstadt → A Bewildering Fate |

Mona trades a reading of the Traveler's fate for help recovering her
master's belongings. This chapter matters because it sets the limits of
astrology in Teyvat: Mona can read everyone's fate, but the fate of the
Traveler — one who comes from beyond this world — does not appear on
Teyvat's starry sky.

### Princeps Cretaceus Chapter — Albedo

*Chapter of Chalk.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Traveler Observation Report (v1.2) | The Rumored Alchemist → The Genius Researcher and the Seed of Another World → The First Experiment: Elements → The Second Experiment: The World → The Third Experiment: Life → The Final Experiment: Withering Glory |

Albedo invites the Traveler to be a research subject in Dragonspine,
calling them outright a "seed from another world". The chain of
experiments on elements, the world, and life reveals that Albedo is an
alchemical creation of Rhinedottir (Gold) — the same being who created
Durin — and that he carries the anxiety of whether he truly counts as
"alive". This is one of the two Story Quests required for Act I of Song of
the Welkin Moon (via *Paralogism*), and one of the few chapters where the
lead character does not appear as a Trial Character.

### Aphros Delos Chapter — Eula

*Chapter of Wave Foam.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | The Spindrift Shall Never Return to the Sea (v1.5) | Shadow of the Past → Speech and Etiquette → Through the Motions, to the Heart |

Jean reports that a descendant of the disgraced Lawrence aristocracy may
be involved with the Fatui, and the best person to investigate is Eula — a
Lawrence who publicly severed ties with her clan. The chapter digs into
the legacy of Mondstadt's old aristocracy and the price descendants pay:
Eula is despised by the whole city for her surname, and chooses to live
"vengefully" by doing what is right.

### Draco Rubedo Chapter — Durin

*(The wiki has not recorded a translation of the chapter name.)* Durin
belongs to Mondstadt/Hexenzirkel per the wiki, but this chapter takes
place in **Nod-Krai** (Lempo Isle).

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | The Magic Known as Storytelling (v Luna III) | A Mother's Last Wish → Interlude: A Moment's Rest → A Prophecy From the Past → Interlude: A Moment's Rest → Fate's Accursed Wiles → Interlude: A Moment's Rest → Revelation Beneath the Stars → Interlude: A Moment's Rest → The Solution of Fate |

Alice summons the Traveler to meet Durin, who is struggling to contain the
Abyss energy inside him (the "Denial of Darkness" form, opposite to
"Confirmation of Purity"). The real task is to finish an unfinished book
by Andersdotter — a late Hexenzirkel member — who left a dying wish that
only Durin could solve it. The journey to find Andersdotter's inspiration
passes through the Mondstadt library (Lisa) and poses the chapter's
central question: how does a character born from the pages of a book go on
living after the words "and they lived happily ever after"?

### Lupus Majoris Chapter — Varka

*(The wiki has not recorded a translation; the Japanese name means roughly
"Chapter of the Heavenly Wolf".)*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | To Those Who Embark on the Expedition (v Luna V) | Triumphant Warrior → Fated Warrior → Lonely Warrior |

The chapter closes the "Varka's expedition" arc running since Version 1.0.
After the northern calamity is extinguished in the Nod-Krai arc, the
expedition prepares to return home but is stopped by a cold wind from the
Abyss carrying a wolf's howl. The chapter ties three threads together —
the Abyss, the Wild Hunt, and Andrius — and opens with Lohen bringing ten
of Varka's letters back to Mondstadt, one of them addressed to Venti.

### Lepus Miles Chapter — Lohen

*(The wiki has not recorded a translation; the Japanese name means roughly
"Chapter of the Knight Rabbit".)*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | A Knight's Legacy (v Luna VII) | A Knight's Merit → A Knight's Pursuit → A Knight's Resolve |

Lohen is the deputy captain of the 5th Company of the Knights of Favonius,
newly returned from the Nod-Krai campaign. The official description poses
the question: is the title of "knight" glory and faith, or just an empty
name before raw power? When a mortal witnesses power great enough to
rewrite fate, should they kneel or stand tall? *(The wiki has not yet
written a plot summary for this chapter.)*

## Liyue

### Trulla Chapter — Xiangling

*Chapter of the Long Dipper.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Mondstadt Gastronomy Trip (v1.0) | Little Chef on a Hunt → Cooking Appointment → Secret Ingredient → Lucky Find → Present From the Past → Cooking Showdown |

Xiangling brings Liyue cuisine to Mondstadt and drags the Traveler along
to gather ingredients. This is the earliest Story Quest to unlock by rank
(AR 12) and the gateway introducing Wanmin Restaurant and her terrifying
habit of culinary "experimentation" — the foundation for countless Liyue
quests to come.

### Fabulae Textile Chapter — Xingqiu

*Chapter of the Brocade.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Bookworm Swordsman (v1.0) | Justice, for Books' Sake → Know Thyself, Know Thy Enemy → Calm Before the Storm → Justice Is Its Own Reward |

The Traveler goes to buy the Liyue volume of the Teyvat Travel Guide and
gets entangled in a case where a big merchant is squeezing a Qingce
Village local's Cor Lapis prices. The investigation reveals the Fatui
behind the order — a textbook example of Fatui meddling in Liyue at the
micro level. Xingqiu answers not with a sword but with commerce: he
mobilizes the Feiyun Commerce Guild to buy out all Cor Lapis on the
market. The chapter also introduces the fallen Guhua Clan.

### Historia Antiqua Chapter — Zhongli

*Chapter of Ancient Stories.* (The chapter name differs from the
constellation name Lapis Dei — one of only two exceptions in the game.)

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Sal Flore (v1.1) | Shadow of Yore → Vortex's Wake → A Ruin Under Lock and Key → Farewell, Sweet Salt → A Record of All Things |
| II | No Mere Stone (v1.5) | Strange Sights, Stolen Souls → Truth in the Stones → Shifting Earth and Lost Threads Come to Light → Amidst Chaos, the Rock Is Unmoved |

Act I revolves around Havria — the Goddess of Salt — murdered by her own
followers; Zhongli helps two "archaeologists" reconstruct the truth and
shows that defeated Archons also leave real descendants and legacies.
Act II continues that motif with a late old friend of Rex Lapis. Both
Acts say the same thing: Zhongli does not keep history to be worshipped
but so that people learn to take responsibility themselves — the reason
he chose to step down from his divine role.

### Sinae Unicornis Chapter — Ganyu

*(The wiki has not recorded a translation; the Japanese name means
"Chapter of the Immortal Qilin".)*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Sea of Clouds, Sea of People (v1.2) | The Illuminated Beast of the Sea of Clouds → A Secretary in a Sea of Humanity → A Pact That Crosses Time |

Ganyu is torn between two halves: her qilin blood bound by an ancient
contract with Rex Lapis, and her very human secretarial work at Yuehai
Pavilion. This chapter confirms the contract remains in force even though
the god who signed it has "died", and Ganyu must decide for herself what
that contract means in an era of human self-rule.

### Alatus Chapter — Xiao

*Chapter of the Golden Winged Peng King.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Butterfly's Dream (v1.3) | The Master of Stars' Deception → Glimmers of the Vigilant Yaksha → The Purging of Evil → Insights of Drifting Dreams |

An Adeptus impostor named "Starsnatcher" uses a Sigil of Permission to
exorcise evil, accidentally drawing karma-corrupted monsters together.
The chapter explains the Yaksha karma mechanism: the corpses of fallen
gods fester into malign energy, the Yakshas were summoned by Rex Lapis to
purge it, and the purging itself corrodes them. Xiao is the last one
left. The ending introduces Pervases — a deceased Yaksha — and the dish
Grilled Tiger Fish becomes how Xiao remembers his comrades.

### Papilio Charontis Chapter — Hu Tao

*Chapter of the Guiding Butterfly.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Yet the Butterfly Flutters Away (v1.3) | The Mysterious Wangsheng Parlor → In Pursuit of a Phantom → Friendships, Wishes, and Regrets → Perfect Send-Off |

Hu Tao helps the trainee Meng resolve his obsession with a dead childhood
friend. The chapter lays down the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor's philosophy:
the boundary between life and death must be kept properly, and
superstition harms more than ghosts do. It also tells the parlor's origin
— the first people who knew how to stop the plague born of Yaksha karma
by purifying the air and cremating the dead, tying straight into Xiao's
chapter.

### Umbrabilis Orchis Chapter — Yelan

*Chapter of the Orchid.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Calculated Gambit (v2.7) | Seven-Star Selection → Candidature Analysis → Foregone Conclusion |

The Qixing's Tianshu (Uncle Tian) has grown old and the selection of his
successor begins. Yelan — the Qixing's covert agent — pulls the Traveler
into vetting three candidates. The chapter shows the dark operational
side of post-Archon Liyue governance: power is transferred through
intelligence and positioning, and even someone thought to be a master
player may turn out to be a mere piece.

### Lagenaria Chapter — Baizhu

*(The wiki has not recorded a translation; the Japanese name means
"Chapter of the Hanging Gourd" — the hanging medicine gourd, symbol of
the physician's trade.)*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | The Heart of Healing (v3.6) | The Search for a Physician → The Quest for a Cure → The Formula for a Miracle |

A mysterious poisoning forces Baizhu to trace it back to the blood of Chi
— the monster once sealed beneath Qingce Village by Rex Lapis. The
chapter digs into the generations-long pact between Baizhu's family and
the white snake Changsheng: trading medical arts for the health of the
pact-bearer themself. The unlock requirements (needing both Hu Tao's and
Xiao's quests) show this chapter was deliberately placed in the same
"price of life" thread in Liyue.

### Grus Serena Chapter — Xianyun

*(The wiki has not recorded a translation; the Japanese name means
"Chapter of the Idle Crane".)*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | A Thousand Moonlit Miles (v4.4) | Leisurely Chatter → A Search for Secrets → Lovely Dreams |

Cloud Retainer leaves Mt. Aocang, taking human form and the name Xianyun
to try living among mortals; the chapter revolves around helping an
elderly woman recover her memories. The content presses on the time gap
between Adepti and humans: to an Adeptus a human lifetime is a blink,
while to humans their memories are everything they have.

## Inazuma

### Grus Nivis Chapter — Kamisato Ayaka

*Chapter of the Snow Crane.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | The Whispers of the Crane and the White Rabbit (v2.0) | A Private Commission → Woven Wishes → Food From Afar → Lasting Promise → With You |

Ayaka asks the Traveler to find an old friend of her mother's, and along
the way gets to live as an ordinary person for the first time. The
chapter shows the price of the Shirasagi Himegimi title: she has carried
the Kamisato name since childhood and has almost no friends. This is one
of the two Story Quests required to continue Archon Quest Chapter II
Act II.

### Carassius Auratus Chapter — Yoimiya

*Chapter of the Ryukin (a goldfish breed).*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Dreamlike Timelessness (v2.0) | The Firework Girl's Strange Stories → Expectations Not to Be Betrayed → A Sliver of Hope → Together Under the Fireworks |
| II | Star-Pickers' Passage (v3.7) | The Seeker of a Meteor Shower → All Things Flawless and Pure → A Flower that Blooms Across the Sky |

Act I takes place during the Vision Hunt Decree: Yoimiya keeps launching
fireworks as a promise not to betray others' expectations, while helping
a fugitive and his guard friend reconcile. Act II takes her abroad for
the first time (Sumeru) to chase a meteor shower. Yoimiya is the first
non-Archon character to get a second Act, and *Star-Pickers' Passage* is
one of the two Act IIs that do not come with a new Weekly Boss.

### Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter — Raiden Shogun

*(The wiki has not recorded a translation; the Japanese name means
"Chapter of the One Who Rules the World".)*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Reflections of Mortality (v2.1) | The Lonesome Euthymia → To Traverse the Mortal Plane → The Fabric of Constancy → To Hear Mortal Hearts |
| II | Transient Dreams (v2.5) | Cleansing Light → Farewell to the Past → Radiant Sakura |

Act I brings the Traveler into the Plane of Euthymia to open the heart of
a god weighing "Eternity" against "Wishes". Act II is one of the most
lore-rich chapters in the game: Rifthounds gnaw the Sacred Sakura's
roots, bringing back memories from 500 years ago and confirming that when
Khaenri'ah fell, monsters also flooded Inazuma, Kitsune Saiguu died in
battle, and Makoto — Ei's elder sister, the previous Shogun — died in
Khaenri'ah. Ei keeps a fragment of Makoto's consciousness, grows the
Sacred Sakura from it, then finally fights her own Shogun puppet, takes
it as her kagemusha, and abolishes the Sakoku Decree.

### Dracaena Somnolenta Chapter — Sangonomiya Kokomi

*Chapter of the Sleeping Dragon.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Warriors' Dreams Like Spring Grass Renewing (v2.1) | Watatsumi Sightseeing → Rumors Abound → Her Secret → Victory Banquet → New Beginning |

After the Vision Hunt Decree ends, rumors that the Fatui and the Tenryou
Commission are plotting to restart the war spread across Watatsumi. The
chapter revolves around Kokomi keeping the peace talks from collapsing,
and reveals her "secret": she is an introvert at heart, exhausted from
having to play spiritual leader for an entire island.

### Taurus Iracundus Chapter — Arataki Itto

*Chapter of the Heavenly Bull.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Rise Up, Golden Soul (v2.3) | The Oni's Evil → The Oni's Justice → The Oni's Pride |

Itto is accused of something he did not do, and believes the culprit is a
fellow oni. This chapter is the clearest statement of the oni people's
place in Inazuman society: descendants of warriors who once served the
Raiden Shogun in war, now facing prejudice and slowly vanishing from the
islands.

### Divina Vulpes Chapter — Yae Miko

*Immortal Fox's Chapter.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | The Great Narukami Offering (v2.5) | Mysterious Urban Legend → Friendship in Writing → A Centuries-Long Dream → Banquet of Parting |

A newly published light novel turns out to retell a true story about
yokai. The chapter traces the centuries-long friendship between Yae Miko
and other long-lived beings, and shows her editor's trade is more than a

### Cypressus Custos Chapter — Kamisato Ayato

*Chapter of the Guardian Deity Cypress.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | The Firmiana Leaf Falls (v2.6) | New Shoots from Old Branches → The Truth Is Also a Lie → The Wind Settles |

A martial tournament between the Hiiragi Clan and the Kujou Clan conceals
a plot to undermine the Kamisato house. The chapter lays bare how
post-Sakoku Inazuman politics works: the three Commissions scramble to
reclaim influence, and Ayato holds the line with schemes rather than
force — a counterweight to his sister's spotless image.

### Acer Palmatum Chapter — Kaedehara Kazuha

*Chapter of the Red Maple.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | A Strange and Friendless Road (v2.8) | Upon the Plain, a Purple Shadow → Through the Mist, a Name Unveiled → Ere the End, a Glance Back |

Kazuha returns to Inazuma and must deal with a cursed sword carrying a
grudge that wants revenge on the Raiden Shogun — a direct remnant of the
Vision Hunt Decree and the death of Kazuha's friend. The chapter closes

### Cisoria Chapter — Chiori

*(The wiki has not recorded a translation; the Japanese name means

|---|---|---|

### Tapirus Somniator Chapter — Yumemizuki Mizuki

*(The wiki has not recorded a translation; the Latin name means roughly
"chapter of the dreaming tapir" — the tapir being the image of the
nightmare-eating baku.)*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | Dream Eater's Melancholia (v5.4) | Encounter in the Realm of Dreams → Crisis at the Aisa Bathhouse → A Deceit Called Happiness → Descendant of Nightmares → Afterword |

The Aisa Bathhouse in Inazuma City is famous for "therapists" who are
actually baku — nightmare-eating yokai — but the bathhouse is in

into the realm of dreams and asks: is eating all of someone's nightmares
really helping them?

## Sumeru

### Vulpes Zerda Chapter — Tighnari

*(The wiki has not recorded a translation; the Japanese name means
"Chapter of the Fennec Fox".)*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
|---|---|---|
| I | The Unanswerable Problems (v3.0) | The Start of Contamination → Into the Hinterland → Heart of the Machine → Garden Memories |

Tighnari traces a strange form of contamination in the forest back to an

the origin of the Hermanubis power Cyno carries. *Oathkeeper* is a rare

### Vultur Volans Chapter — Alhaitham

*Chapter of Heaven Falcon.*

| Act | Act name | Sub-quests |
