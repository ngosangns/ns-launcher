# World Quest — Fontaine

*The nation of water: from the sunken empire of Remuria and the Narzissenkreuz
secret society, to the Melusine villages beneath the sea.*

Fontaine has the densest concentration of World Quests in the game to date.
The wiki counts **211 World Quests** belonging to Fontaine, of which **199**
sit in the main World Quest section (136 permanent quests + 63 event-linked
quests) and **12** are Reputation Requests. Fontaine has no separate "Random
World Quests" or "Random Events" groups.

Its geographic scope covers: the Court of Fontaine Region, Belleau Region,
Beryl Region (home to Merusea Village and Elynas — the exposed remains of the
giant beast of the same name), Liffey Region (Fortress of Meropide), Morte
Region (Tower of Ipsissimus), Erinnyes Forest, Nostoi Region with the town of
Petrichor, the Sea of Bygone Eras (the ruins of Remuria), Romaritime Harbor,
and the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering Region (the
new Institute area, version 5.x). All flooded areas are scattered across these
regions rather than split into a zone of their own.

Four opening series — Ancient Colors, Ann of the Narzissenkreuz, Unfinished
Comedy, and the hinge segment at the Institute of Natural Philosophy —
converge into a single grand narrative: the **Narzissenkreuz Ordo**, a secret
society founded over 400 years ago by Rene de Petrichor and Jakob Ingold at
the Tower of Ipsissimus. The Ordo's goal was to use the power of the
Primordial Sea to merge the wills of countless people into a single Descender,
so as to survive the apocalypse prophesied for Teyvat.

## Ancient Colors ("Seymour's Story: Prologue")

The big opening series in the Fontaine wilds begins when the Traveler steps
into a scuffle between a band of Eremites and a Melusine named Mamere.
Following her underwater, the Traveler reaches Merusea Village — the
underground settlement where the Melusine live — and meets Seymour, a
worn-out, badly damaged "robot dog" the villagers treat as a friend. After
helping Mamere gather "paint colors" for her picture, the Traveler runs into
the Eremites again, this time alongside a man named Jakob. The next leg is a
hunt for four Strange Parts scattered across the Beryl Region so Serene can
repair Seymour, and it closes with a chase after Jakob himself. Fontaine's
Reputation menu calls this series "Seymour's Story: Prologue".

## Ann of the Narzissenkreuz ("Ann's Story: Prologue")

In a Fontaine that should have no Oceanids left, the Traveler meets a tiny
Oceanid named Ann. Ann lives entirely inside her own imaginary world — one
with a "Narzissenkreuz Adventure Team" and a "Princess" who needs rescuing —
and pulls the Traveler into that game until the door to the Annapausis opens.
The finale takes the player to the "real Annapausis" and to Mary-Ann, a name
that keeps returning throughout the rest of the saga. The wiki notes this
series is thick with references to *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* and
*Through the Looking-Glass*: from the Jabberwock's Holy Sword to the
achievement "Ann in Wonderland".

## Unfinished Comedy ("Caterpillar's Story: Prologue")

This series plays out entirely inside the Fortress of Meropide. Caterpillar
is a human-looking being who calls himself a "monster"; Lanoire is a girl
born inside the prison itself, with no legal identity, and so is forever
forbidden to leave. Seeking atonement and self-understanding, Caterpillar
befriends Lanoire and resolves to take her back to her family on the surface.
The plan hinges on the "Game of the Rich" contest run by the guard Estienne,
whose prize is a supervised trip above. The guard Noailles — a stickler for
the rules — deploys Gardemeks to stop them, but in the end both escape and
Lanoire is reunited with her grandfather Earnshaw.

## In the Wake of Narcissus

The big series that decodes the entire mystery. The group deduces that the
Annapausis is the work of an Oceanid connected to Mary-Ann Guillotin — most
likely Lyris, once the director of the Narzissenkreuz Institute orphanage. At
the tower in the Annapausis, they must defeat Al and Jak, who are being
controlled by Mary-Ann, then use "faith and imagination" to rebuild the path
to the door of the "real Annapausis". Caterpillar then explains the
Narzissenkreuz and the society's goal, and proposes finding the Holy Blade of
Narzissenkreuz — both to settle the Mary-Ann matter and to break the Ordo's
plan. Four Ordo-marked sites in the Morte Region, each holding one
ingredient, form four side quests. The finale unfolds beneath the Primordial
Sea, where the Traveler directly confronts the "dream of salvation" the
Narzissenkreuz left behind.

## Canticles of Harmony — Remuria Beneath the Sea of Bygone Eras

Fontaine's second big series opens in the town of Petrichor in the Nostoi
Region with a string of strange phenomena. Following a cat named "Osse" into
the Faded Castle, the Traveler descends to the Sea of Bygone Eras — the
sunken capital of **Remuria**, a civilization older than even the Hydro
Archon Egeria's time. Remuria was built by the god Remus (whom the people
called the God King); after the golden age of Pax Remuria, it was Remus's own
attempt to dodge the prophecy of destruction that brought the empire down:
he split authority among four mortal Harmosts, then mixed primordial water
with an immortal stone to create Golden Ichor, pouring his subjects' souls
into stone golems. Once the Traveler learns that "Monsieur Os" is really
Cassiodor, they hunt down Boethius, restore the golden aqueduct system to
enter the "Domus Aurea", and end everything there.

## Fontaine Research Institute Chronicles

An overloaded Arkhium core in an experiment led by Edwin Eastinghouse blew
the Fontaine Research Institute sky-high. The researcher Desnos recruits the
Traveler into the site survey team — after a long, drawn-out paperwork
process. The wreckage is full of out-of-control meka and leaking Arkhium; the
deeper the investigation goes, the clearer it becomes that the Institute hid
how dangerous the first explosion really was. A second explosion caused by
carelessness shows the Institute's management hasn't improved. Two follow-up
quests shift into a manhunt for Nacker and the Arkhium Kinetic Core, and
reveal that Edwin — presumed dead — is still alive, though mentally unstable
and endlessly repeating the number "five".

## Questioning Melusine and Answering Machine

Fontaine's detective case. Sir Arthur — the steambird mascot standing in
front of The Steambird's offices — has vanished. Talochard, a Melusine of the
Marechaussee Phantom, volunteers to investigate alongside Agent Curve, a
Suppression Specialist Mek built by the scientist Mosso Lombroso. Four
suspects emerge from the scene: the drunkard Rocher, the boy Kevin, the
Elegant Tableware Confrerie scam ring, and finally Baulard — a peripheral
Fatui intelligence officer hunting for Mosso's research facility. It was
Baulard who took the threatening letter signed "Crow or Blackbird?", in which
the writer admits to kidnapping Sir Arthur and demands the paper clear
Mosso's name. By act three, all four trails are ruled out and the newspaper
is tangled in a brand-new problem.

## Scope Notes

- The wiki has no pages named "Aquatic Aberrants", "Ancient Colossus",
  "Sirocco", or "The Wondrous Adventure of Baby Bird" in the Fontaine quest
  category. The "wrathful fish" and Fisharium quests actually belong to the
  Sunny Summer Fontinalia event; "Ancient Colossus" corresponds to the
  **Elynas** area — the exposed remains of the beast of the same name in the
  Beryl Region; and "Beneath the Light of Jadeite" is a Liyue series (Chenyu
  Vale), not Fontaine's.
- Hydro Sigils in Fontaine are an exploration reward, not a quest series; the
  wiki has no World Quest filed under that criterion.
- Fontaine has no Random World Quests or Random Events sections of its own:
  intersecting Category:Fontaine Quests with those two categories returns
  nothing.
- Archon Quests, character Story Quests, Commissions, and Domains are outside
  this file's scope.
