# World Quest — Natlan, Nod-Krai, and Snezhnaya

*Updated through Version 7.0 ("Everwinter Without Mercy", released 08/12/2026).*

Teyvat's three newest lands are built to three very different rhythms.
Natlan is the volcanic land of six tribes, where World Quests mostly revolve
around Saurians, Phlogiston, and an ancient promise between humans and
dragons. Nod-Krai is a frozen archipelago beneath the Moon's shadow, where
most of the main storyline is packaged as World Quest Series rather than
Archon Quests. Snezhnaya is the Fatui's homeland, and its world-quest branch
ties into the Shadow Realm, biological alchemy, and the legacy of the Belyi
Tsar.

This document follows the exact sectioning of the wiki's "World Quest" page:
the Natlan section, the Nod-Krai section (with its Intel Quests subsection),
the Snezhnaya section, Adventure Rank Ascension, and Other. Quests that only
exist inside limited-time events live on the separate "Event List" tab and
are not listed here, except for a few cases the wiki still files under
Category:World Quests.

Total quests listed: **106 Natlan**, **141 Nod-Krai** (including 8 Intel
Quests), **56 Snezhnaya**, **5 + 4 Ascension**, **8 Other**.

its sanity when it matures. Removing the mark requires two **Golden
Entreaties**, and each big series below is one leg of that search.

## Natlan — Between Pledge and Forgettance

The series that opens the Golden Entreaty arc. The Traveler is pulled into a
strange space, meets and names a baby Saurian — Little One. To enter the
ruins of the Sage of the Stolen Flame, the two must obtain the Children of
Echoes' token, even if it means dueling the tribe chief Pacal. Inside the
ruins, the Traveler and Little One swap perspectives to solve crystal
puzzles and cross lava. The murals along the way retell the ancient oath
between the Sage and the dragon Xbalanque — the lore foundation for all of
Natlan. At the end of the series, the pair obtains one of the two Golden
Entreaties.

## Natlan — Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm

Natlan's biggest series, split into four acts, set in the ash-covered ancient
city of Ochkanatlan. The Traveler chases a vicious dragon that stole a
treasure, befriends the adventurer Bona and the creature Cocouik, then climbs
aboard the flying ship Tonatiuh to find the "Jade of Return". The truth
surfaces in act three: the jade is actually the second Golden Entreaty, and
the legend around it was fabricated by Kukulkan to lure people in. The dragon
is Och-Kan, the former king of Ochkanatlan, warped by poison; his memories
show he rejected Kukulkan's plan for a new civilization built on the ruins
of both humans and dragons. Bona turns out to be the daughter of Taika — the
expedition leader who died to this very trap years ago.

## Natlan — Chronicler of the Crumbling City

Inside the volcano lies a dark ancient city, not hot at all as one would
imagine. A "guide" possessing a Secret Source Servant offers to take the
Traveler to the Heart of the Sacred Mountain, but passing the gate requires
a permit from the Chamber of Deliberation and reactivating the Celestial
Axis at the city's center. Most of the series is spent cleansing the Abyss
corruption eating away at four old ruin sectors — the court of judgment,
the calculation array, the force-reversal sector, and the armory — each one
a sub-quest. The wiki has not written a full plot summary for this series

## Natlan — In the Footsteps of the Chosen of Dragons

Not a linear arc but a collection: fifteen quests scattered across the six
tribes, doable in any order, all revolving around Little One growing up amid
everyday Natlan life — fishing, modeling for film, delivering gifts, finding
friends, learning to fly. Only when they're tallied up does it emerge that
the curse can only be cleansed at the Heart of the Sacred Mountain, and the
final quest unlocks. At the end, Little One is freed and chooses to go
adventuring on its own; the Traveler receives a permanent small Saurian for
the Serenitea Pot.

## Snezhnaya — In the Dwelling of Life

A seven-part series and Snezhnaya's heaviest storyline. In Morepesok, the
Traveler takes over Jary's contract to blast a tunnel into the abandoned
town of Okurov. Inside the tunnel, they find Nastya — a little girl with a
tree branch growing out of her head — and bring her "home". Okurov turns
out to be a place where the townsfolk willingly turned themselves into
trees under the hand of "Doctor Moreau", believing that becoming a single
family would end all pain and conflict; those who refuse to merge are
called "Badbugs" and burned as fertilizer. Watching over the town is Zhara,
the winged creature from the "firebird" legend. The Eye of Graeae confirms
all of this traces back to Koshchei the Deathless, the biological alchemist
who helped create its own body.

## Snezhnaya — On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb

The heaviest lore series on Snezhnaya's history. Following the Eye of
Graeae's guidance, the Traveler finds the Pale Crown Palace beneath the
roots of the Tsar's White Birch — at once the residence and the tomb of the
Belyi Tsar, and also where the Eye of Graeae was created, inside the
underground research facility Hvergelmir. This was where the Belyi Tsar
welcomed both humans and fae, and where the Kresnik flame was first kindled;
he fell in battle against the Abyss about 500 years ago, part of his body
becoming a birch tree. On the prototype train into the palace, the Traveler
is separated from Paimon and falls into Hvergelmir's memories: two
researchers, Izmaylov and Azazel, are monitoring a "Proto-Man" inside an
egg, fearing that the newly crowned Tsaritsa's ban on alchemy will bury the
Belyi Tsar's unfinished work. Part two continues hunting the remaining
power fragments inside the tomb.

## Other

The "Other" section of the "World Quest" page currently holds only 8 quests:
seven belonging to the **Temple of Space** and one unlocking the Travelers'
Tales system. The Temple of Space is an area with its own map and
exploration progress, floating in the sky above Windrest Peak (Mondstadt),
so the wiki assigns it the "Other" region instead of a nation.

### The A Long Day in the Mountains Series

The Traveler investigates rumors of a "ghost" at the Old Sanatorium Site and
meets Zeitlind, a girl searching for her missing sister — the grave bearing
a face identical to hers turns out to be her sister's. The Watcher Nihilita
appears and asks the Traveler to free the Temple of Space from its
"inverted fate": the temple, created by the God of Space, has been seized by
the remaining Watcher, Melanta, who terrorizes its inhabitants. Three
exhibition halls — Luyang Academy, the Mahavaipulya Chamber, the Desert
Pavilion — are freed one by one, each returning a keepsake of the sister
Raumhilda. To reach the Atrium of Suchness and face Melanta, someone must
sacrifice their entire memory to rebuild the "Path to the Heavens"; Hilda
did exactly that long ago, and in the end Zeitlind chooses to walk her
sister's path. Melanta is defeated and self-destructs; Zeitlind returns to
the surface with blanked memories and a stele carved with the words "To be
continued".

## Scope Notes

- The wiki appends the suffix "(Quest)" to page titles when they collide
  with another page. This document records quest names as they appear in
  game, i.e. without the suffix: *Saurian Sojourn*, *Stand by Me*, *Witch's
  Lodge*, *Moonlit Patrol Exercise*, *Variations on Belyi and Chernyi*,
  *The Chosen One's Promise*, *Another Horizon of Adventure*.
- *The Unmanned Vessel (I)/(II)/(III)* and *Within the Sequence* are
  unofficial names coined by the wiki for disambiguation; in game they share
  one name or have no separate display name.
- Natlan and Snezhnaya have no Random Events, Repeatable Random Quests, or
  Reputation Requests sections on the "World Quest" page — that part is
  hidden in comments, meaning the two regions' Reputation systems haven't
  been tabulated on the wiki (not documented on the wiki). Same for
  Nod-Krai, which has the Intel Quests branch instead.
- Many new series (*Chronicler of the Crumbling City*, *A Finale
  Emberforged*, *From One Case to Another*, *Nightingale's Song*, *Moon
  Gazing*, *Beyond Silver and Fine Gold*, *Colors of Emptiness*, *Polkka
  Beneath the Moon's Oracle*, *The Unmanned Vessel*, *Mean Streets of the
  Despicable*, *Her Palace Collapses Into the Blizzard*) currently have no
  Summary section on the wiki; the summaries here are built from quest
  descriptions and each sub-quest's intro line.
- *A Scrollbound Skirmish* is an Event Quest of the Legends Ablaze:
  Cross-Border Brawl event but still sits in Category:World Quests and
  Category:Natlan Quests, so it's kept in the list.
- The Natlan Archon Quests (Chapter V), the Luna/Nod-Krai arc, and the
  Snezhnaya arc are outside this document's scope.
