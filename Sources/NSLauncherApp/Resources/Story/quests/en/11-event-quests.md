# Event Quests

*Every quest tied to a limited-time event, ordered by game version.*

Event Quests are quests that only open while an event is running. Most are
flagged as World Quests, but flagship events usually come with their own
Story Quest chain — and this is exactly where much of the game's most
notable side lore lives, since it sits in neither Archon Quests nor
character Story Quests.

**Updated through Version 7.0** (the current version per the wiki at lookup
time). This document lists **639 quests** across **158 events** that have
quests, plus the two *Travelers' Tales* rounds (80 short vignettes) and a
note on *Perilous Trail*.

A note on version numbering: after Version 5.8, the game didn't continue
into 6.x but used the **"Song of the Welkin Moon"** branch named Luna I –
Luna VIII (equivalent to the Version 6 line), then moved to Version 7.0.

## The Annual Festivals

This group helps with quick lookup: the same festival carries a different
event name each year, so it's easy to get lost when searching by name.

**Lantern Rite (Liyue, Lunar New Year)** — the most regular annual festival:
Version 1.3 *Lantern Rite* (+ the *Lantern Rite Tales* side chapter) → 2.4
*Fleeting Colors in Flight* → 3.4 *The Exquisite Night Chimes* → 4.4
*Vibrant Harriers Aloft in Spring Breeze* → 5.3 *Springtime Charms* → Luna
IV *A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon*. Each edition gifts a free 4-star
character and usually ties to a newly released Liyue character.

**Windblume Festival (Mondstadt, spring)** — 1.4 *Invitation of Windblume* →
3.5 *Windblume's Breath*. The wind-flower festival, heavy on poetry and
music.

**Weinlesefest (Mondstadt, grape harvest)** — 3.1 *Of Ballads and Brews*.

**Moonchase Festival (Liyue, mid-autumn)** — 2.1 *Moonlight Merriment*.

**Inazuma festivals** — 2.6 *Hues of the Violet Garden* (Magnificent Irodori
Festival) → 3.3 *Akitsu Kimodameshi* (summer test of courage) → 5.4
*Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival* (Mikawa Flower Festival) → Luna VII
*Phantasmal Pals*.

**Sabzeruz Festival (Sumeru, Nahida's birthday)** — 5.1 *Chromatic Ode of
Candies and Roses*.

**Summer flagship events (usually with a temporary map)** — 1.6 *Midsummer
Island Adventure* and 2.8 *Summertime Odyssey* (Golden Apple Archipelago) →
3.8 *Secret Summer Paradise* (Veluriyam Mirage) → 4.8 *Summertide Scales and
Tales* (Simulanka) → 5.8 *Sunspray Summer Resort* (Tenochtzitoc) → Luna VIII
*Sunny Summer Fontinalia* (the Fontinalia film festival).

**Permanently kept event chapters** — three events the wiki files under
"Permanent Event Chapters", meaning they remain playable after the event
ends: *Sunspray Summer Resort* (5.8), *Sunny Summer Fontinalia* (Luna VIII),
and *A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon* (Luna IV). Some older chapters
like *Lantern Rite Tales*, *Theater Mechanicus: Stage of Wonders*, *Gliding
Challenge*, and *Energy Amplifier Initiation/Fruition* were also once kept
in the quest menu.

**Events that recur across multiple runs (not season-bound)** — Windtrace
(1.5, 2.4, 3.3), Lost Riches (1.2, 2.0, 3.0, 4.3), Hyakunin Ikki (2.1, 2.5,
3.1), Vibro-Crystal (2.6, 3.5, 4.6), Fayz Trials (3.0, 3.7, 4.5), Divine
Ingenuity (2.5, 3.7), Spices From the West (2.6, 3.5), Outside the Canvas
Inside the Lens (2.6, 3.2, 4.8), Mementos of Teyvat (5.0, 5.8), Bing-Bang
Finchball (4.8, Luna III), Travelers' Tales (5.4, 5.7), Reminiscent Regimen
(2.8).

## Scope Notes

- The list is taken from the wiki's Category:Event Quests (639 quests).
  Content that launched alongside an event but is classified differently by
  the wiki isn't counted — most notably *Perilous Trail* (2.7), filed as an
  Archon Quest.
- The two *Travelers' Tales* rounds (5.4, 5.7) contain 80 short vignettes;
  the wiki doesn't file them under Category:Event Quests but they were only
  playable during their events, so they're listed above.
- Events with only rewards/login content (web events, test runs,
  pre-downloads) have no quests and don't appear in this document.
- For a few consecutively numbered chains (Custom Gift Envelope No. 1–7,
  The Recollector's Path Part 1–6, Receiver of Friends From Afar Part I–IV),
  names are abbreviated into ranges to avoid repetition.
