# Commissions and Repeatable Quests

*Teyvat's daily chores: commissions, random events, reputation requests —
the content few people retell but that takes up most of the playtime.*

Beyond Archon Quests, Story Quests, and World Quests — the one-time-only
chains — Genshin has a layer of quests that repeat daily, weekly, or on
random encounters. This layer includes: **Commissions** (daily commissions
from the Adventurers' Guild), **Random Events** and **Repeatable Random
Quests** (encountered while traveling), **Requests** and **Bounties** from
the reputation system, **Crimson Wishes** in Dragonspine, and a few smaller
groups. This document lists every name, grouped by region.

Wiki totals: **61 Basic Commissions**, **184 NPC Commissions** (not counting
2 removed event commissions), **22 Random Events**, **16 Repeatable Random
Quests**, **57 Reputation Requests**, **5 Crimson Wishes**.

## Daily Commissions — Mechanics

### Receiving and Completing

- Unlocked at **Adventure Rank 12**, after completing *Every Day a New
  Adventure*.
- At each **Daily Reset**, the player receives **4 commissions**: usually
  3–4 Basic Commissions and 0–1 NPC Commissions, drawn randomly from the
  commission pool of the currently selected region.
- Unfinished commissions **do not carry over** to the next day; they're all
  replaced at reset. Two identical commissions can appear on the same day.
- A commission **occupies** the monsters and objects (including chests) in
  its area until it's completed and the zone reloads. Conversely, if an NPC
  or location is occupied by another quest, a commission needing that same
  NPC/location won't appear — for example, while Noelle's Hangout is in the
  training-ground scene, the commission *Ellin, the Wannabe Knight* is
  blocked.
- In Co-Op, players can help with as many commissions as they like, but each
  person still only **claims rewards up to 4 times per day**.

### Choosing a Preferred Region

The Adventurer Handbook has a **Preferred Region** setting: pick one
unlocked region to concentrate your commissions there; the default is
"Random". Only **Areas** already filled into the map (by activating the
corresponding Statue of The Seven) can be chosen as locations. The setting
takes effect from the next reset. Some emergency commissions and NPC
follow-up commissions can still fall outside the preferred region.

### Rewards

- Reward amounts are **calculated from the Adventure Rank at the moment the
  commission is assigned**, not when it's completed.
- Most commissions pay the standard bundle (Adventure EXP, Mora,
  Companionship EXP); some non-combat NPC commissions pay a reduced bundle;
  some NPC commissions also pay extra unique items outside every reward
  cap.
- **Bonus reward**: after claiming all 4 daily rewards, return to any
  Adventurers' Guild branch to collect **20 Primogems**, 500 Adventure EXP
  (none at AR 60), AR-scaled Mora (from ~5,575 up to 18,175), Companionship
  EXP, plus an **Adventure Treasure Pack** containing 2-star character
  ascension materials. This bonus can't be claimed in Co-Op.
- **Story Keys**: from AR 26 up, each completed commission adds 1 point; 8
  points trade for 1 Story Key to unlock Hangout Events. You can hold at
  most 3 keys, but since the counter keeps running to 8, you can effectively
  "hold" a fourth key.
- **Adventure Encounters**: Encounter points earned from quests, chests,
  items, and events can be exchanged directly for commission rewards. Those
  commissions can still be played but won't pay a second time.

### Cycle Theory — the NPC Commission Rotation

Each region has its own NPC Commission pool. Each commission is "seeded" a
fixed number of times per cycle; completing one removes it from the pool
until the cycle ends. Unfinished commissions stay in the pool, and **the
cycle doesn't end as long as one NPC Commission remains uncompleted**.
Basic Commissions don't affect this rotation. Estimated cycle lengths (if
no NPC commission is skipped): Mondstadt ~80 days, Liyue ~60, Inazuma ~40,
Sumeru ~40, Natlan has no data yet.

Exception: commissions tied to an **incomplete achievement** have a much
higher appearance rate and **bypass the cycle limit** — repeating without
limit until the achievement is earned. If an achievement requires several
different commissions, the whole group is exempt from the limit.

### Co-Op Restrictions

Only the host can trigger commissions involving Ley Line Monoliths,
Transport Balloons, and Slime Balloons, and only the host can advance
dialogue steps. Commissions requiring item turn-ins (and *Pigeons Go AWOL*)
can't be started or progressed in Co-Op. Companionship EXP is doubled with
2 or more players.

## Random Events (22)

Encounters while traveling, shown as a **blue banner** on screen and only on
the mini-map — absent from the big map and the Quest Menu. Walk too far or
hesitate too long and the event disappears. They can be forced to spawn by
quitting the game near a spawn point and logging back in. After **50 random
events** in one reset cycle, they stop spawning.

Rewards can only be claimed **up to 10 times per day** (shared with
Repeatable Random Quests): Companionship EXP ×10–15, Mora ×1,055–1,936, Fine
Enhancement Ore ×0–2, Mystic Enhancement Ore ×0–2. Companionship EXP is
doubled in Co-Op with 2+ players.

The content is almost always clearing a monster cluster (hilichurls, slimes,
Treasure Hoarders, Fatui) or destroying a device; a rare exception is
*Friend to Animals* (feeding a dog). **Fontaine, Natlan, and Nod-Krai have
no Random Events.**

## Repeatable Random Quests (16)

The wiki calls this group **Random World Quests**: they are real World
Quests — with a name shown at the left of the screen, listed in the Quest
Menu, and navigable — but they only appear randomly while exploring, and
also vanish if you hesitate or stray too far. They share the 10-rewards-per-day
cap with Random Events. **Liyue, Fontaine, Natlan, and Nod-Krai have none of
these.**

## The Reputation System and Requests

### Mechanics

**Reputation** (City Reputation) is a per-nation progress track. It requires
**Adventure Rank 25** plus completing one Archon Quest and one unlock World
Quest for that region:

| Region | Archon Quest | Unlock World Quest | Responsible NPC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mondstadt | The Outlander Who Caught the Wind | Knight of the Realm | Hertha |
| Liyue | Farewell, Archaic Lord | Ministry Missions | Ms. Yu |
| Inazuma | The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia | The Commission's Commission | Madarame Hyakubei |
| Sumeru | The Morn a Thousand Roses Brings | Corps of Thirty Recruitment | Effendi |
| Fontaine | As Light Rain Falls Without Reason | Steambird Interview | Euphrasie |
| Natlan | Flowers Resplendent on the Sun-Scorched Sojourn | — | See Tribe Reputation |
| Nod-Krai | Step 16 of Where the Moon Rises | — | See Meeting Point |
| Snezhnaya | Multiple quests in Everwinter Without Mercy | — | See Meeting Point |

The main sources of Reputation EXP are **Bounties** (target hunts, weekly)
and **Requests** (item requests or monster-camp cleanups, weekly), plus map
exploration and regular quests. Reputation rewards include cooking recipes,
forging blueprints, furnishing blueprints, namecards, and a wind glider.
Once a region's reputation level is maxed, you stop earning EXP and can no
longer do that region's Bounties/Requests.

**Bounties** unlock at Reputation Level 2 (Level 1 in Natlan) and pay
120/160/200/240 Reputation EXP by difficulty. At most **3 bounties per
week**, shared across all nations. In Mondstadt and Liyue you must use
Elemental Sight to follow three clues before the target appears (10-minute
limit); in Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, and Natlan the target appears as soon
as you enter the hunting zone, usually with escort monsters.

**Requests** unlock at Reputation Level 2 and are handled like World Quests,
so navigation works. At most **3 requests per week shared across all
nations** (including Natlan's Supply Notices). Each request pays **80
Reputation EXP + 20,000 Mora** — the 80 figure was raised from 40 in version
"Luna III". There are 3 *Hero-in-Training* achievements for the 3/6/9
request milestones.

### New-Region Variants

- **Natlan — Supply Notices**: replaces Requests. Turn in Natlan specialty
  materials (Cacahuatl, Candlecap Mushroom, Embercore Flower, Grainfruit,
  Spinel Fruit, Brilliant Chrysanthemum, Quenepa Berry, Saurian Claw
  Succulent, Sprayfeather Gill, Glowing Hornshroom, Skysplit Gembloom,
  Dracolite) at a tribe's Obsidian Totem Pole. Each turn-in pays **160
  Reputation EXP for that tribe + 2 Sanctifying Unction**, sharing the
  3-per-week cap with other regions' Requests.
- **Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya — Meeting Points**: a separate reputation system
  split by location rather than by nation. Nod-Krai has 6 points
  (Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop, Frostmoon Enclave, Final Night Cemetery,
  The Witch's Garden, Favonius Keep, Cliffwatch Camp); Snezhnaya has 3
  (Tidesong Cavern, Huntsman's Cabin, The Korolevskiy Theater).

## Crimson Wish Quests (5)

A **Dragonspine**-exclusive system tied to the Frostbearing Tree. It unlocks
when the tree reaches **Level 8** and stops working when the tree hits
**Level 12** (surplus Crimson Agates are kept). Every **Monday and Friday**
server time, 5 Crimson Wishes spawn scattered across Dragonspine; each pays
**1 Crimson Agate**. Doable in Co-Op — helpers also receive agates if
they've unlocked the system in their own world — but still capped at 5
agates per reset.

| Name | Content |
|---|---|
| Ancient Wind | Timed challenge collecting Anemo particles spawned by the ley line |
| Dredging the Land | Break Frostburst barrels within a short time to unblock the ley line |
| Icy Harvest | Clear out the monsters fighting over the agates |
| Strange Shifts | Destroy the ancient ruins' automated defense mechanisms within a short time |
| Liquidation | Defeat the Snezhnayan recon squad probing the region's special energy |

## Other Repeatable / Periodic Quest Groups

### Ascension Quests (5) — Adventure Rank

Not repeatable quests, but they sit in the same "system quest" group and
block progress if skipped. The wiki lists 5 pages: *Adventure Rank
Ascension 1*, *Adventure Rank Ascension 2*, *Adventure Rank Ascension 3*,
*Adventure Rank Ascension 4*, and *World Level Ascension*. Until the
corresponding ascension quest is done, surplus Adventure EXP is converted
to Mora at a 1:10 rate once you have enough EXP for AR 60.

### Ascension Challenges (4) — Genius Invokation TCG

A group of World Quests inside the TCG system, required to reach the next
**Player Level** and awarding the **Player Badge**: *Ascension Challenge I*,
*II*, *III*, *IV*. Introduced in Version 3.3. (Note the distinction from
the Adventure Rank Ascension Quests above — two completely different
systems.)

### Intel Quests (8) — Nod-Krai

Unlocked by trading **5 Recorded Intel** per quest at Katheryne's Intel
Exchange counter in Nod-Krai. Only members of the Curatorium of Secrets
faction can gather Recorded Intel — currently the two characters Nefer and
Jahoda. Four base quests: *The Vanishing Bounty Target* · *The Disturbance
Caused by Theft* · *The Haunted Pirate Shipwreck* · *The Suspicious Hermit*.
Finishing all four opens *The Dust Settles* (automatic), plus *The Man Who
Once Lied* (if you own Nefer) and *They Who Abandoned the Past* (if you own
Jahoda); finishing the latter two then opens *Collaborative Commission*.

### Position in the Classification Tree

The World Quest page files the following groups as **sub-branches of World
Quests**: Random Events, Reputation Requests, Ascension Quests, Crimson
Wish Quests (Dragonspine), and Intel Quests (Nod-Krai). Commissions are a
**separate quest type**, not part of World Quests.
