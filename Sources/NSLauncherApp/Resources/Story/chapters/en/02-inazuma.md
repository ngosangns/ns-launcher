# Chapter II — Inazuma

*A goddess hidden behind a puppet, and the price of unchanging eternity.*

Inazuma is an island nation closed off from the world by the Sakoku
Decree, under the rule of the Electro Archon — the Raiden Shogun. The
moment the Traveler sets foot there, they are caught up in the nation's
greatest scandal: the Vision Hunt Decree, confiscating every Vision in the
country. Ostensibly meant to stamp out personal ambition, it is in truth a
scheme pulled by the Fatui together with corrupt officials, designed to
weaken Inazuma and pave the way for Delusion trafficking — false Visions.

## The Puppet and the True God

Yae Miko reveals the core secret: the "Raiden Shogun" who appears before
the public is only a puppet made in the true god's image. The real god —
Raiden Ei — withdrew into a private plane of existence, honing her
swordsmanship for hundreds of years and all but abandoning governance.
Stepping into that realm, the Traveler learns that Ei once had a twin
sister, Makoto, who fell in the incident tied to the fall of Khaenri'ah
500 years ago — a loss that shaped Ei's extreme obsession with
"unchanging eternity".

## Watatsumi Island Rises

On Watatsumi Island, a resistance force led by Sangonomiya Kokomi has
quietly opposed the decree for years. In parallel, a truth is exposed: the
Kujou Clan itself — father of the loyal general Kujou Sara — colluded with
the Fatui for profit, a shock that forces Sara to confront her own
father's betrayal.

> **Turning point.** The final confrontation between the Traveler and the
> Shogun is not meant to destroy her, but to test whether Ei can look back
> at the path she chose. She agrees to repeal the decree, admits her
> mistake in how she understood "eternity", and begins to rule through her
> own true presence instead of hiding behind the puppet.

> **Open mysteries.** The Fatui's true role in Inazuma remains incomplete.
> What exactly happened in the Khaenri'ah incident that killed Makoto? To
> what extent does the Shogun puppet persist as an independent being now
> that Ei has retaken power?

## Side Story: Enkanomiya

Lying deep beneath the sea off Watatsumi, Enkanomiya was once the island
of Byakuyakoku before it sank into the ocean even before the Archon War.
Its people were once terrorized by the Bathysmal Vishaps, so they created
the Dainichi Mikoshi — an "artificial sun" — to bring back the light. The
land's turning point came when the ancient serpent god Orobashi, defeated
on the surface, fled down here and was invited to rule in place of the old
rulers. Completely cut off from the world above, Enkanomiya gradually
became a society frozen in time. Orobashi was later condemned by Celestia
itself for the crime of "offending the heavens" — the same familiar
pattern: any power that dares establish its own order outside the system
of the Seven is punished or isolated.

## Character Profiles

**Yae Miko** — A nine-tailed kitsune, the Raiden Shogun's oldest and most
trusted friend. Her story reveals five centuries of loneliness: after the
companions of her era (Makoto among them, before her sacrifice to become
the Archon) passed away or vanished one by one in the incident 500 years
ago.

**Kujou Sara** — After discovering her adoptive father colluded with the
Fatui to profit from the Vision Hunt Decree, Sara is forced to redefine
her loyalty: no longer blind obedience to a bloodline or an order, but
loyalty to the Shogun's ideal of "eternity".

**Kamisato Ayaka & Ayato** — Because of her position as the clan's public
face, Ayaka cannot openly oppose the Vision Hunt Decree, so she secretly
aids those who lost their Visions; Ayato uses political maneuvering to
defuse power struggles within the nobility without direct confrontation.
Together they show the Kamisato Clan operating on two parallel tracks:
openly compliant, quietly resisting injustice.

**Arataki Itto** — His story draws on the folk legend of the crimson oni
and the blue oni, through which he helps another oni descendant realize
that oni today have every right to decide their own fate, no longer bound
by curses or duties from the past.

## Character Profiles (Additional)

**Sangonomiya Kokomi** — An extended quest chain after the Archon Quest:
as peace talks between Watatsumi and the Tenryou Commission are about to
take place, Kokomi discovers some Tenryou soldiers are still being
approached by Fatui remnants seeking revenge — showing the Fatui's
aftermath in Inazuma is not fully over after the Archon Quest.

**Kaedehara Kazuha** — The Kaedehara Clan was once a distinguished vassal
of the Kamisato Clan but declined after its estate was confiscated: a
hundred years ago, Kazuha's grandfather encountered a wanderer calling
himself Kunikuzushi (the young Scaramouche himself) — who defeated them
and threatened to destroy their family swordsmanship; the Kamisato Clan
ordered the incident hushed up, leaving Kazuha's family to bear the blame.
When the Vision Hunt Decree was issued, Kazuha watched his close friend
challenge the Shogun and be slain, so he left Inazuma and joined Beidou's
fleet in Liyue.

*(Yoimiya, Sayu, Shikanoin Heizou, Kuki Shinobu, Gorou, Thoma, Kirara:
mostly only slice-of-life Hangout Events, revealing no important lore tied
to Khaenri'ah/Celestia/Fatui.)*

## World Quests (Additional)

**Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual** — A world quest chain on Narukami
Island, unlocked after Chapter II Act I of the Archon Quest. The Traveler
and a mysterious shrine maiden cleanse the corrupted Sacred Sakura, tied
to the faith of the Yashiro Commission — one of the longest and most
elaborate world quests in Inazuma.

**Chinju Forest — Ioroi and the Kitsune Saiguu** — Ioroi, a tanuki
bake-danuki who once protected the forest, often rivaled the divine fox
Kitsune Saiguu — the goddess who once kept the Grand Narukami Shrine. When
Saiguu fell in the Cataclysm 500 years ago (after staging a game of
hide-and-seek to keep Ioroi away from danger), Ioroi never knew, firmly
believing his friend was merely hiding; when she never reappeared, he
raged and wreaked havoc across Narukami Island to "lure" Saiguu out, and
was finally sealed away. The story reinforces that the Cataclysm 500 years
ago took not only Makoto but many of Inazuma's other guardian gods and
beasts.

**Tatara Tales** — At Tatarasuna, the Mikage Furnace — a smelting forge
specializing in Jade Steel, the material behind the signature sharpness of
Inazuman blades — runs on the energy of the Tatarigami (vengeful
spirits/corrupted energy). When war broke out between the Shogunate and
the Sangonomiya resistance, this energy source spun out of control,
dragging the two opposing sides into conflict right there — an example of
Inazuma's military technology and the consequences of abusing corrupted
energy in a civil war.
