# Chapter I — Liyue

*The rumored death of a god, and the truth of a millennia-old contract.*

As soon as the Traveler arrives in Liyue, they witness a shocking event:
Rex Lapis — the Geo Archon, who has governed this land for thousands of
years — falls in the middle of a public rite. The whole city is thrown
into turmoil, and the Traveler is swept into a web of power made up of the
Qixing merchant council led by Ningguang, the mysterious adepti, and
Childe (Tartaglia) — a young Fatui Harbinger, belligerent yet strangely
straightforward.

## Zhongli's Secret

For his own ends, Childe introduces the Traveler to Zhongli — the quiet
consultant of a funeral parlor, who takes charge of organizing Rex Lapis's
funeral rites. Through this, the truth emerges: Zhongli is Morax himself,
the mortal vessel of the Geo Archon. The death was only a facade for him
to step down from his divine role and hand Liyue's fate over to its
people.

## The Ancient God Awakens

Evidence of Fatui interference gradually comes to light. Racing to stop
Childe from stealing the Geo Gnosis, the Traveler watches him summon
Osial — the ancient sea god Morax himself sealed away millennia ago.
Nearly all of Liyue's forces must join hands: the adepti operate ancient
weaponry, the Millelith hold the defensive line, and Ningguang makes the
boldest decision of her life — sacrificing her own Jade Chamber to sink
the ancient god back to the bottom of the sea.

> **Turning point.** After the battle, the truth is laid bare: Zhongli
> willingly handed his Gnosis to another high-ranking Harbinger — not
> because he lost, but as a calculated move that left even Childe shut out
> of the Fatui's real plan. Liyue officially enters the era of human
> self-rule.

> **Open mysteries.** What ultimate goal are the Fatui collecting Gnoses
> for? How does Celestia — the heavenly realm behind the Archons — truly
> operate? Why did Zhongli choose to relinquish his divine authority at
> this exact moment?

## Side Story: The Chasm

The Chasm was originally a site where the Fatui were invited by the Qixing
themselves to cooperate in mining, after a mysterious "black mud" was
discovered underground — a manifestation of Abyss-corrupted energy. The
Fatui once built a machine hoping to purify this corruption, but it
overloaded and was destroyed, forcing the mine to shut down. Deeper still,
excavated ancient memories reveal that a nameless yaksha once fought
alongside the Millelith against the Abyss right here during the Cataclysm
500 years ago — turning the Chasm from an ordinary mine into an ancient
battlefield showing that the disaster's reach extended far beyond
Khaenri'ah alone.

## Character Profiles

**Xiao** — The last surviving yaksha of the five Morax summoned after the
Archon War to purge the chaotic remnants of the fallen gods. Constantly
taking lives caused the yakshas to accumulate "karmic debt" that gnawed at
them from within; the other four all succumbed to it. Xiao's solitude is
the direct price of keeping Liyue's long peace.

**Zhongli** — His own story delves deeper into the Archon War era and the
role of the adepti under his command, while reinforcing that handing the
Gnosis to the Fatui was a calculated move — marking Liyue's official
transfer of power from gods to humans.

**Hu Tao** — The 77th-generation director of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor,
a funeral house founded by a single family line since the Archon War.
Raised on the boundary between life and death, she sees death as natural
rather than something to fear — while Zhongli, the parlor's most senior
consultant, quietly knows every secret behind that culture.

## Character Profiles (Additional)

**Ganyu** — A half-human, half-qilin illuminated beast (a kind of
adeptus), she has quietly watched over Liyue Harbor for 3,700 years and
once fought beside Rex Lapis in the Archon War — now serving as the
Qixing's secretary, a bridge between the ancient world of the adepti and
the modern administrative machine.

**Qiqi** — A corpse revived by the adepti as a zombie, living under
Baizhu's care. Unlike most zombies, who must obey the one who woke them,
Qiqi awoke on her own and so commands herself; her "three souls" were
severed, leaving only a single incomplete fragment held stable by the
talisman on her forehead — the reason for her severe memory loss.

**Baizhu** — Heir to a generations-old contract with the white snake
Changsheng: in exchange for secret medicinal arts, the contract-bearer
pays with their own health (Baizhu's predecessors died young because of
it). A mysterious poisoning case in his own quest traces back to the blood
of the beast Chi — a creature once sealed deep beneath Qingce Village by
Rex Lapis himself.

**Shenhe & Xianyun (Cloud Retainer)** — Shenhe is Cloud Retainer's
disciple, taught exorcism arts after the adeptus found her single-handedly
defeating the remnant of a dead god. Cloud Retainer chose to take human
form and the name "Xianyun" to try blending into human society.

**Beidou** — From a wandering orphan, she grew into the captain of the
Crux fleet, famous for her heroic spirit — she once personally slew the
sea monster Haishan.

**Keqing** — The Yuheng of the Qixing, openly skeptical of the worship of
Rex Lapis, believing humans must decide their own fate rather than entrust
it to gods and adepti — an ironic conviction that the gods themselves hold
in high regard.

**Xingqiu** — Second son of the Feiyun Commerce Guild; his own quest shows
the Fatui once meddled in a small trade price-fixing scheme in Liyue, an
example of Fatui interference in everyday Liyue life at the micro level,
not just grand politics.

**Yanfei** — A half-adeptus like Ganyu, but her father is a divine beast
symbolizing justice (a xiezhi) who once campaigned with Rex Lapis. Born in
peacetime, Yanfei never signed a contract with Rex Lapis as Ganyu did — a
counterpoint showing that not every adeptus descendant carries the weight
of ancient oaths.

## World Quests (Additional)

**Guizhong & the Guizhong Ballista** — Guizhong, the "God of Dust", once
led the Guili Plains and allied with Morax in the pre-Archon War era. She
designed the prototype of an ancient ballista (the Guizhong Ballista) —
the very weapon the Qixing later reused against Osial in the Archon Quest.
Guizhong fell in the Archon War itself, showing that a dead god's
technological legacy still protects Liyue millennia later.

**Chenyu Vale** — A region tied to ancient dragon lineages and the story
of Changsheng (Baizhu's snake), expanding the lore of longevity pacts and
the bond between ancient dragons/beasts and the people of Liyue — the same
"price of immortality" theme already seen through Baizhu.

**The Nine Pillars of Peace (Cuijue Slope)** — Nine pillars carved with a
philosophical inscription: every conflict in the world springs from human
desire (greed, envy, wrath, pride, rivalry...), erected to remind the
people of Liyue not to fall into those desires and make war on one another
— embodying the founding philosophy of Rex Lapis's era: peace maintained
through moral education, not divine power alone.

**Hereafter** — A follow-up to Xiao's Story Quest: a young man enamored
with adeptus legends finds an ancient relic and impersonates an adeptus
for fame, gradually consumed by greed, before repenting and seeking
atonement by restoring the temple of a fallen yaksha — a personal story of
redemption, loosely tied to the world of the adepti.

*(A Lone Ship in Guyun — introducing Beidou's Crux fleet at the ancient
battlefield of the Guyun Stone Forest — is slice-of-life in nature, with
no major lore reveals.)*
