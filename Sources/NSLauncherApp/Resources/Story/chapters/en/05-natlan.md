# Chapter V — Natlan

*A land where death is not quite the end — until that flame begins to
fade.*

Natlan is the land of the Pyro Archon Mavuika — the only Archon to win
the throne through a tournament 500 years ago, rather than being
appointed by Celestia like the other Archons. The land runs on the "way
of competition": tribes compete for glory, and the souls of the dead can
be revived through the Sacred Flame if they leave behind enough battle
honors.

## The Corroded Flame

When the young companion Kachina falls in battle, the resurrection ritual
unexpectedly fails. The cause: the Sacred Flame itself — the foundation
of Natlan's entire resurrection system — had been quietly corroded from
within by the Abyss for a long time. Facing the risk of the whole system
collapsing, Mavuika sacrifices part of her own divine power to
temporarily sustain the flame, knowing in advance the price is a weakness
she cannot hide.

## The Oath of 500 Years Ago

Capitano — the first Harbinger and commander of the Fatui army — appears,
challenging Mavuika over an oath she has not fulfilled. Though she
overwhelms and gravely wounds him, Mavuika still reveals that her power
has severely weakened. Notably, Capitano refuses to seize her Gnosis
dishonorably, out of respect for the victory she once won over him in the
past — showing that even the Fatui have their own boundaries of honor.

> **Turning point.** The Abyss launches its final all-out strike: a false
> dragon form called the "Lord of Eroded Primal Fire." Six heroes
> representing Natlan's tribes join forces with Mavuika and the Traveler,
> carrying the message throughout: unity rises above rivalry — the core
> lesson Natlan wants to convey, in stark contrast to the culture of
> competition that shaped the whole land.

## The Night Kingdom

The story advances to the "Night Kingdom" act: to permanently stabilize
Natlan's ley lines, Mavuika must prepare to offer her own life to the
entity ruling that kingdom — Yohualtecuhtin (Ronova). At the decisive
moment, Capitano unexpectedly steps forward and offers his own eternal
life as a substitute, since Ronova accepts his life's value as equal to
an Archon's. Because Capitano was already cursed with immortality, he
merges his existence with the Lord of the Night instead of truly dying,
stabilizing Natlan without anyone having to truly sacrifice their life —
one of the few times a Fatui Harbinger acts on ideals almost entirely
opposite to the usual villain image.

> **Open mysteries.** What does the Abyss really want in targeting the
> Sacred Flame? What was the oath of 500 years ago between Mavuika and
> Capitano, and why did he accept that price? Can Natlan's weak Ley Lines
> hold out long-term, or is this only a temporary fix before a greater
> threat from the Abyss?

## Character Profiles

**Ororon** — Possessed by a Khaenri'ahn soul named Guthred, leading to a
direct confrontation with Capitano. Through the process of reclaiming
control of his body, he is recognized as a bearer of one of the ancient
"Ancient Names."

**Kinich** — A Saurian hunter who discovers ancient dragon ruins and
meets Ajaw, the "dragon king" surviving from the era of dragons millennia
ago. The two strike a symbiotic deal: Ajaw lends his power, and in
exchange, when Kinich dies, his body will belong to Ajaw.

**Xilonen** — The smith responsible for forging an "Ancient Name" for the
Traveler. Through her story, a worrying truth is revealed: Natlan's Ley
Lines are inherently very weak, leaving the fate of the entire land
almost entirely dependent on the Night Kingdom.

## Character Profiles (Additional)

**Kachina** — The Traveler's first guide upon arriving in Natlan,
introducing the "Pilgrimage" culture — a ritual every Natlan child grows
up dreaming of joining to become a warrior defending the nation. Her
ongoing inner conflict: "does someone like me deserve an Ancient Name?" —
a question that grows heavier after she is captured by the Abyss, the
event that caused the resurrection ritual to fail (covered in the main
section).

**Citlali** — When the Abyss launches its invasion, she discovers an
Abyss operative seducing a member of another tribe to probe for
information about "Mare Jivari" — a mysterious place/concept related to
the Abyss; she rescues the victim but lets the mastermind escape.

**Chasca, Iansan, Mualani, Varesa** — Each tied to a different Natlan
tribe (Flower-Feather Clan, Collective of Plenty, People of the Springs),
mainly portraying the culture and symbiotic bonds between Natlan's people
and the Saurians, with no major new reveals for the overall storyline.

## World Quests (Additional)

**Tribal Chronicles** — Instead of the traditional regional reputation
system, each of Natlan's six tribes has its own world quest chain,
unlocked after completing Act I of Chapter V. Each tribe has 3 story
chapters, the last usually being the story quest of a representative
character (as listed above). Most of the deep Abyss/Ley Lines content
still lies in the Archon Quest itself and personal story quests, not in
the tribes' everyday world quests.

**Yupanqui's Turnfire — "Kinich's Deal" (Scions of the Canopy)** — Kinich
and the Traveler investigate a ritual that drains Abyssal energy gnawing
at an ancient entity/dragon named Kongamato, but this method will almost
certainly kill it. A tribal elder objects, viewing Kongamato as a
historical symbol — a conflict between "eradicating the Abyss" and
"preserving ancestral heritage." The quest also reveals Kinich once fell
into a hidden dimensional space called the "Beastly Rifts" while
pursuing an Abyss-corrupted creature.

**"Hoarse Echoes" (Children of the Echoes)** — The Traveler seeks out a
former master artisan who left the craft to care for a sick relative — an
everyday story about personal sacrifice rather than expanding the
Abyss/Ley Lines mysteries.

## Expansion Regions (content released after Natlan's launch)

**Ochkanatlan — "Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm"** — The Traveler and
companions head to the ancient city of Ochkanatlan seeking the legendary
"Jade of Return," meeting an explorer amid the ruins. Along the journey,
they discover a mineral containing abnormally powerful fire, before
confirming they have reached an important ancient site/entity tied to the
city buried in ash.

**Atocpan — "Chronicler of the Crumbling City"** — A large-scale quest
chain leading the Traveler into the Tollan volcano to explore the secrets
within, confronting Abyss forces and solving many layers of puzzles
before unlocking a battle involving an ancient dragon machine — one of
Natlan's largest world quests to date, arguably a true "interlude" rather
than just an ordinary world quest.
