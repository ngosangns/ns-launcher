# Chapter VII — Snezhnaya

*The final destination of the journey — and a place where even death must
follow regulations.*

Snezhnaya is the land of eternal winter at the northern tip of Teyvat,
worshipping the Tsaritsa — the Cryo Archon, whose true name is
**Anastasya Feodorovna Snezhnaya**. She was not appointed by Celestia but
succeeded the Belyi Tsar after that Archon perished in the Cataclysm.
Right afterward she did three things that shaped the entire nation:
founded the Fatui with Pierro as its first member, wiped out the
political power of the fae aristocracy, and replaced it with the
**Snezhnograd Veche** — a council headed by humans.

This is the most technologically advanced place in Teyvat. Every
settlement is built around a **Kresnik Torch**, powered by Kresnik
Kristall ore; without it, the whole town freezes. That is also why, when
the Tsaritsa orders nationwide energy cuts to feed a research project,
the cold stops being a matter of weather and becomes a matter of
politics.

## The One Who Walks Into Winter

The Traveler comes to Snezhnaya for two reasons: this is where the
Tsaritsa is gathering the Gnoses, and it is the final stop in the
sibling's parting words. But Paimon falls ill almost the moment they
cross the border — she senses an alien power radiating from the direction
of the **Armory Palace**, and it is wearing her down.

The first person they meet is **Pierro**, Director of the Eleven
Harbingers, quietly tucking a blanket around Paimon inside a tent. He
does not give his name, only tells them to care for her, then leaves —
promising to meet again. His four-pointed star-shaped pupils confirm his
Khaenri'ahn origins.

The second is the **Eye of Graeae**, an ancient machine that recognizes
the Traveler as a Descender and offers help in exchange for an
unspecified favor.

## Someone Is Responsible for the Cold

In the town of Sretomorozsk, the hunter **Alyosha** explains **Project
Stuzha** to them: the Tsaritsa's plan to cut energy across Snezhnaya in
order to redirect all the savings to a vital line of research. The direct
consequence is that ordinary people do not have enough Kristall to heat
their homes.

The Traveler and Alyosha's investigation pulls out a Kristall
embezzlement ring between a local merchant and the mayor himself. Both
are arrested — by a Fatui officer who almost certainly had a share in it.
This is the tone of the whole chapter: in Snezhnaya, the one who exposes
corruption and the one who handles it are usually the same machine.

Alyosha is also the gateway to **Rokot** — an underground organization
opposing the Fatui, operating on the principle of anonymous members, no
meetings, each choosing their own way of working. The Traveler is
inducted.

## The Veche and What Goes Unsaid

In the capital Snezhnograd, the Traveler meets **Sandrone** again — the
one who died in Nod-Krai. She was originally an automaton built by Alain
Guillotin in the image of his late younger sister, so her core was
restored in Fontaine; Pulonia was not, and in her place now stands a new
machine named Fagio. **Childe** has also become a "councilor" in the
Veche under Capitano's arrangement, and he visibly hates the job.

Energy researcher **Mitya** reveals the thing closest to the truth: the
project needs an entirely new energy source, drawn from something called
the **Stellar Linchpin**, and the process is devouring Kristall at a
near-zero progress rate, leaving the whole research team so drained they
drift about like soulless husks. When he is about to say what that energy
is for, the scarf around his neck tightens on its own, cutting the
sentence short.

At the Veche session, Pulcinella presents the figures and declares
Project Stuzha will continue; Childe objects to the allocation,
Pantalone counters that fairness is relative. The project passes with
61% — a narrower margin than last time. An industrial magnate votes
against it against all predictions, because he was threatened by
**Lelek**. Lelek specializes in breaking into the most heavily guarded
places just to leave a threatening letter, and his sole goal is to end
Project Stuzha.

## A Night at the Theater

The dress rehearsal of a classical ballet at the Korolevskiy Theater is
trapped by a snowstorm. The next morning, two people are dead — no
wounds, no signs of struggle. The following night, two more, exactly as
the Traveler saw in a dream of a spiral staircase chased by black mist.

In the middle of the investigation, **Odette** publicly reveals that she
is Lelek — at exactly the moment doing so makes her the prime suspect.
But this way of killing is not hers.

The truth emerges layer by layer, and every layer is the same kind: a
guest with no ticket turns out to be the ghost of a mercenary who died
over 400 years ago; the "Tonia" sitting in the audience is actually named
Ptashka, a little girl who died of Frostblight and was seen as a daughter
by a father who had lost his own; Mitya died at the Armory Palace a week
ago — he simply does not remember.

> **Turning point.** The last one unmasked is Alyosha — or rather, the
> thing wearing his face inside the theater: **Ronova**, the Ruler of
> Death, one of the Four Shades. She admits to everything, kills two more
> people with a snap of her fingers to prove her authority, then explains
> the rules: each day she is only permitted to take two lives, because
> the angels' construction interfering with the theater's Ley Lines has
> obstructed her soul-guiding work.

## The Death Curse

The mechanism is stated outright: **whenever a technological
breakthrough powerful enough to threaten Celestia appears, the "Death
Curse" activates** — everyone in the area dies temporarily and loses all
memory of it after reviving. Not a punishment — just maintenance. The
entire tragedy in the theater began only because Mitya sat watching the
ballet and thought of a research idea.

That is also the explanation for the "soulless daze" of the Armory Palace
research team: they have died and come back many times without knowing
it.

Ronova comes to the Armory Palace to take Paimon, and Pierro blocks her
hand. She drops only one remark: Paimon originates from Celestia but
carries not a shred of its power or authority. The Traveler fights back
and manages only a single Anemo scratch before she leaves.

> **Turning point.** Pierro explains why he took Paimon away: to heal
> her. Paimon was once "pulled" from Celestia down to Khaenri'ah and
> offered to King Irmin as a gift — a king who did not care at all.
> Meaning she has existed for at least 500 years without keeping a single
> fragment of memory. To Pierro, she is one of the last lucid things left
> of Khaenri'ah. It is also here that the goal of the Tsaritsa and the
> Fatui is spoken aloud, no longer speculation: **to overthrow the
> Heavenly Principles**. Pierro shows the Traveler the secret weapon kept
> for that day.

Project **Mistilteinn** is suspended until Ronova is eliminated. At the
end of the chapter, the Tsaritsa returns to Snezhnograd after a campaign
to destroy the monster causing the endless snowstorms in the east,
accompanied by the Druzhna guard. She does not say a single word, and
walks straight into the palace.

> **Open mysteries.** What do Project Stuzha and Mistilteinn actually
> produce, and why does it need an energy source outside Teyvat's entire
> elemental system? What is the weapon Pierro keeps? What became of
> Anastasya's son — the ancestor of the Khaenri'ahns — and what does that
> make the Cataclysm in her eyes? The Pyro Gnosis vanished along with
> Irminsul, so what meaning does the plan to gather all seven Gnoses
> still hold? And Paimon: a thing from Celestia, no power, no memories,
> always at the Descender's side — who pulled her down, and for what?
>

## Character Profiles

**Alyosha** — A hunter, a Rokot member, the Traveler's true guide in
Snezhnaya. He cares for a senile old woman simply because they share
roots in an abandoned town. Ronova choosing his face to wear is a
calculated act of cruelty.

**Odette** — Ballet dancer, Fatuus, and Lelek. Her father was a Fatuus
who died in the early phase of what later became Project Stuzha; her
mother was a famous ballet dancer who broke her leg from exhaustion and
never danced again. She joined the Fatui for the money, under Capitano's
policy, arranged by Signora at Arlecchino's suggestion. At the end of the
chapter she visits Signora's grave and chooses to live on with both
identities.

**Vodyanitsa** — Lead singer of the troupe, a Water Imp, Alyosha's Rokot
teammate. In the theater, she is the only one who actively pulls the
Traveler out of the realm of death and takes on the "guardian angel" role
before her own turn comes.

**Mitya** — Energy specialist of the Armory Palace, the only man who died
yet kept going to work, going to the theater, and taking notes. The red
eye symbol — Ronova's mark — he drew in blood on his own soul so he would
not forget again.

**Pierro** — Appears at the start of the chapter as a stranger tucking a
blanket around Paimon, and at the end as the only one who dares stand in
front of a Shade. The longest-surviving fragment of Khaenri'ah, and the
mouthpiece for the Tsaritsa's true ambition.

**Tsaritsa** — Has yet to speak a single line. Everything known is
indirect: she rules directly rather than letting go like most other
Archons, rarely uses her own power because she "has never acknowledged or
accepted it," and according to Columbina she is as transparent as ice on
a lake — you can see through her, but you cannot measure the bottom.

**Ronova** — The chapter's antagonist, but not exactly a villain: she is
simply enforcing the regulations, including the one that only permits
killing two people a day. The most notable detail: a Descender's soul
cannot be brought into the Ley Lines, and she declares she has no
intention of getting involved with a Descender.
